An FA(3) invoice most often fails validation because of a wrong namespace, element order, missing fields, NIP and date formats, too many decimal places, a `P_12` rate, or incomplete `Adnotacje`. Each of the 12 errors below has a cause and XML you can copy.

Validation messages are opaque. Instead of "missing delivery date" you get `cvc-complex-type.2.4.b: element P_6 not found`. If your invoicing software generates the XML itself, it should catch most of these. The explanations help when you integrate with KSeF (Krajowy System e-Faktur, Poland's national e-invoicing system) through the API, write a custom exporter, or want to know why an invoice was rejected. The examples are based on the FA(3) schema in force since 1 February 2026.

## 1. Missing or wrong namespace

The `<Faktura>` element must carry the FA(3) namespace. Without it the validator recognises no element and reports an error on every one. The usual causes are a template from the internet that uses FA(2), an omitted `xmlns` attribute, or a typo in the URI (`2025/06/25/13775` is easy to get wrong). A namespace from templates predating February 2026 is almost certainly outdated, because FA(2) stopped applying on 31 January 2026.

<XmlExample copyable>
  ```xml
  <!-- ❌ No namespace -->
  <Faktura>
    ...
  </Faktura>
  <!-- ❌ Old FA(2) namespace, invalid since 1 February 2026 -->
  <Faktura xmlns="http://crd.gov.pl/wzor/2023/06/29/12648/">...</Faktura>
  <!-- ✅ Correct FA(3) namespace -->
  <Faktura xmlns="http://crd.gov.pl/wzor/2025/06/25/13775/">
    ...
  </Faktura>
  ```
</XmlExample>

## 2. Wrong element order

The schema uses `xs:sequence`, so element order is mandatory. `Podmiot2` placed before `Podmiot1` rejects the whole file. Software normally keeps the order, but manual edits and custom exports break it.

At the top level: `Naglowek` → `Podmiot1` → `Podmiot2` → [`Podmiot3`] → [`PodmiotUpowazniony`] → `Fa` (including `FaWiersz`) → [`Stopka`] → [`Zalacznik`]. Elements in brackets are optional, but if present they have a fixed place. `FaWiersz` sits **inside** `Fa`, not next to it.

<XmlExample copyable>
  ```xml
  <!-- ❌ Podmiot2 before Podmiot1 -->
  <Faktura xmlns="http://crd.gov.pl/wzor/2025/06/25/13775/">
    <Naglowek>...</Naglowek>
    <Podmiot2>...</Podmiot2>
    <Podmiot1>...</Podmiot1>
    <Fa>...</Fa>
  </Faktura>
  <!-- ✅ Correct order -->
  <Faktura xmlns="http://crd.gov.pl/wzor/2025/06/25/13775/">
    <Naglowek>...</Naglowek>
    <Podmiot1>...</Podmiot1>
    <Podmiot2>...</Podmiot2>
    <Fa>...</Fa>
  </Faktura>
  ```
</XmlExample>

The same rule applies inside `Naglowek` (`KodFormularza` → `WariantFormularza` → `DataWytworzeniaFa`), `Podmiot1`, `Podmiot2` and `Fa`. The full order is in the [FA(3) XSD schema](https://crd.gov.pl/wzor/2025/06/25/13775/schemat.xsd).

## 3. Missing required elements

FA(3) has dozens of mandatory fields at different levels, and the validator names the missing one. The easiest to forget are `WariantFormularza`, `KodWaluty`, `P_15` and `RodzajFaktury`. The minimum:

- **Naglowek:** `KodFormularza` (with attributes `kodSystemowy="FA (3)"` and `wersjaSchemy="1-0E"`), `WariantFormularza`, `DataWytworzeniaFa`
- **Podmiot1/DaneIdentyfikacyjne:** `NIP`, `Nazwa`
- **Podmiot1/Adres:** `KodKraju`, `AdresL1`
- **Podmiot2/DaneIdentyfikacyjne:** `NIP` (or `KodUE` + `NrVatUE`, or `KodKraju` + `NrID`), `Nazwa`
- **Podmiot2:** `JST`, `GV`
- **Fa:** `KodWaluty`, `P_1`, `P_2`, `P_15`, `Adnotacje`, `RodzajFaktury`

<XmlExample copyable>
  ```xml
  <!-- ❌ Missing WariantFormularza -->
  <Naglowek>
    <KodFormularza kodSystemowy="FA (3)" wersjaSchemy="1-0E">FA</KodFormularza>
    <DataWytworzeniaFa>2026-09-15T10:00:00Z</DataWytworzeniaFa>
  </Naglowek>
  <!-- ✅ All required fields in Naglowek -->
  <Naglowek>
    <KodFormularza kodSystemowy="FA (3)" wersjaSchemy="1-0E">FA</KodFormularza>
    <WariantFormularza>3</WariantFormularza>
    <DataWytworzeniaFa>2026-09-15T10:00:00Z</DataWytworzeniaFa>
  </Naglowek>
  ```
</XmlExample>

## 4. Wrong NIP format

The schema requires exactly 10 digits with no separators. `123-456-78-90` and `PL 1234567890` are both wrong. The `PL` prefix goes in a separate field (`PrefiksPodatnika` in `Podmiot1`, `KodUE` in `Podmiot2`).

<XmlExample copyable>
  ```xml
  <!-- ❌ Hyphens in NIP -->
  <NIP>123-456-78-90</NIP>
  <!-- ❌ Country prefix in NIP -->
  <NIP>PL1234567890</NIP>
  <!-- ✅ 10 digits, no separators -->
  <NIP>1234567890</NIP>
  ```
</XmlExample>

<Warning>
  A Polish buyer's NIP must go in the `NIP` field of `Podmiot2/DaneIdentyfikacyjne`, not in
  `NrVatUE` or `NrID`. In the wrong field the invoice will not be delivered correctly in KSeF.
</Warning>

## 5. Wrong date format

The Polish `15.09.2026` instead of `2026-09-15` is the most common slip. The second is mixing up date fields (`P_1`) with the date-time field (`DataWytworzeniaFa`). Date fields (`P_1`, `P_6`, `P_6A` and others) take `YYYY-MM-DD`. `DataWytworzeniaFa` needs `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ` with the `Z` (UTC) suffix.

<XmlExample copyable>
  ```xml
  <!-- ❌ Polish date format -->
  <P_1>15.09.2026</P_1>
  <!-- ❌ Date and time in a date-only field -->
  <P_1>2026-09-15T10:00:00Z</P_1>
  <!-- ✅ Correct date -->
  <P_1>2026-09-15</P_1>
  <!-- ❌ Missing Z suffix -->
  <DataWytworzeniaFa>2026-09-15T10:00:00</DataWytworzeniaFa>
  <!-- ❌ Timezone offset instead of Z (the MF information sheet requires Z) -->
  <DataWytworzeniaFa>2026-09-15T10:00:00+02:00</DataWytworzeniaFa>
  <!-- ✅ Date and time with Z (UTC) -->
  <DataWytworzeniaFa>2026-09-15T10:00:00Z</DataWytworzeniaFa>
  ```
</XmlExample>

## 6. Too many decimal places

ERPs and spreadsheets store numbers at full precision (`123.456789`), and the excess ends up in the XML on export. Round to:

- **amounts** (P_11, P_13_x, P_14_x, P_15): max **2** places,
- **quantities** (P_8B): max **6** places,
- **unit prices** (P_9A, P_9B): max **8** places.

The decimal separator is always a dot. A comma or a space as a thousands separator is not allowed.

<XmlExample copyable>
  ```xml
  <!-- ❌ 3 decimal places in P_15 (max 2) -->
  <P_15>123.123</P_15>
  <!-- ✅ Correct, 2 places -->
  <P_15>123.12</P_15>
  <!-- ❌ 7 places in quantity P_8B (max 6) -->
  <P_8B>1.1234567</P_8B>
  <!-- ✅ Correct, 6 places -->
  <P_8B>1.123456</P_8B>
  <!-- ❌ Comma as decimal separator -->
  <P_15>123,45</P_15>
  <!-- ❌ Space as thousands separator -->
  <P_15>1 234.56</P_15>
  <!-- ✅ Dot only, no separators -->
  <P_15>1234.56</P_15>
  ```
</XmlExample>

## 7. Missing Podmiot2, JST and GV

`JST` and `GV` did not exist in FA(2), so an export based on an old template does not generate them. The schema always requires them, even when the value is `2` (not applicable). Add them at the end of `Podmiot2`, after `Adres` and any optional fields.

- `JST` = `1`: the invoice concerns a **subordinate** local government unit; `2`: not applicable.
- `GV` = `1`: the invoice concerns a VAT group member; `2`: not applicable.

<XmlExample copyable>
  ```xml
  <!-- ❌ Missing JST and GV -->
  <Podmiot2>
    <DaneIdentyfikacyjne>
      <NIP>9876543210</NIP>
      <Nazwa>Firma Nabywcy</Nazwa>
    </DaneIdentyfikacyjne>
    <Adres>
      <KodKraju>PL</KodKraju>
      <AdresL1>ul. Testowa 1, 00-001 Warszawa</AdresL1>
    </Adres>
  </Podmiot2>
  <!-- ✅ With JST and GV -->
  <Podmiot2>
    <DaneIdentyfikacyjne>
      <NIP>9876543210</NIP>
      <Nazwa>Firma Nabywcy</Nazwa>
    </DaneIdentyfikacyjne>
    <Adres>
      <KodKraju>PL</KodKraju>
      <AdresL1>ul. Testowa 1, 00-001 Warszawa</AdresL1>
    </Adres>
    <JST>2</JST>
    <GV>2</GV>
  </Podmiot2>
  ```
</XmlExample>

## 8. Wrong VAT rate in P_12

`P_12` accepts only values from the FA(3) catalogue, and they are case- and space-sensitive. A bare `0` does not exist, you must give a variant (`0 KR`, `0 WDT`, `0 EX`), the space in `0 KR` is mandatory, and `zw` is lowercase.

Allowed: `23`, `22`, `8`, `7`, `5`, `4`, `3`, `0 KR`, `0 WDT`, `0 EX`, `zw`, `oo`, `np I`, `np II`.

<XmlExample copyable>
  ```xml
  <!-- ❌ Bare 0, no variant -->
  <P_12>0</P_12>
  <!-- ✅ Domestic 0% rate -->
  <P_12>0 KR</P_12>
  <!-- ❌ Percent sign -->
  <P_12>23%</P_12>
  <!-- ✅ No percent sign -->
  <P_12>23</P_12>
  <!-- ❌ Uppercase -->
  <P_12>ZW</P_12>
  <!-- ✅ Lowercase -->
  <P_12>zw</P_12>
  <!-- ❌ Missing space -->
  <P_12>0KR</P_12>
  <!-- ✅ With space -->
  <P_12>0 KR</P_12>
  ```
</XmlExample>

The value `oo` (reverse charge) applies only to domestic transactions. For invoices to foreign buyers who account for VAT in their own country, use `np I` or `np II`.

## 9. Wrong identification of a foreign buyer

FA(3) identifies the buyer differently depending on the country:

- **Poland:** the `NIP` field (10 digits, no prefix),
- **EU:** `KodUE` + `NrVatUE` (country code separate, VAT number without prefix),
- **outside the EU:** `KodKraju` + `NrID`.

A common mistake is putting the country code inside the VAT number (`DE123456789` in `NrVatUE`). `KodKraju` in `DaneIdentyfikacyjne` is about tax identification, and `KodKraju` in `Adres` is about the address. They are two separate fields.

<XmlExample copyable>
  ```xml
  <!-- ❌ Country prefix in VAT number -->
  <DaneIdentyfikacyjne>
    <NrVatUE>DE123456789</NrVatUE>
    <Nazwa>Deutsche Firma GmbH</Nazwa>
  </DaneIdentyfikacyjne>
  <!-- ✅ EU buyer: country code in a separate field -->
  <DaneIdentyfikacyjne>
    <KodUE>DE</KodUE>
    <NrVatUE>123456789</NrVatUE>
    <Nazwa>Deutsche Firma GmbH</Nazwa>
  </DaneIdentyfikacyjne>
  <!-- ✅ Non-EU buyer -->
  <DaneIdentyfikacyjne>
    <KodKraju>US</KodKraju>
    <NrID>12-3456789</NrID>
    <Nazwa>American Corp</Nazwa>
  </DaneIdentyfikacyjne>
  ```
</XmlExample>

## 10. P_15 doesn't match the totals

`P_15` (total amount due) must equal the sum of the net amounts (`P_13_x`) and the tax (`P_14_x`). The usual culprit is rounding (tax of `22.9977` rounded to `22.99` instead of `23.00`) or a skipped `P_13_x` field, such as `P_13_6_1` for the 0% rate.

Formula: **P_15 = sum of all P_13_x + sum of all P_14_x**, that is P_13_1 to P_13_11 and P_14_1 to P_14_5. Fields P_13_6_1 to P_13_11 (0% rate, exempt, reverse charge) have no matching P_14_x, but their net amounts count towards P_15.

<XmlExample copyable>
  ```xml
  <!-- ❌ P_15 doesn't match the sum -->
  <P_13_1>1000.00</P_13_1>
  <P_14_1>230.00</P_14_1>
  <P_15>1200.00</P_15>
  <!-- ✅ Correct sum: 1000 + 230 = 1230 -->
  <P_13_1>1000.00</P_13_1>
  <P_14_1>230.00</P_14_1>
  <P_15>1230.00</P_15>
  <!-- ✅ Mixed rates: 100 + 23 + 200 + 16 = 339 -->
  <P_13_1>100.00</P_13_1>
  <P_14_1>23.00</P_14_1>
  <P_13_2>200.00</P_13_2>
  <P_14_2>16.00</P_14_2>
  <P_15>339.00</P_15>
  ```
</XmlExample>

The [ksefuj.to](/en) validator tolerates a difference of up to 0.01 PLN when comparing totals.

## 11. Incomplete Adnotacje

The name suggests notes, but `Adnotacje` is a mandatory section with eight fields, each of which needs a value. Three (`Zwolnienie`, `NoweSrodkiTransportu`, `PMarzy`) need nested elements even when the answer is "no". The order is mandatory too: P_16 → P_17 → P_18 → P_18A → Zwolnienie → NoweSrodkiTransportu → P_23 → PMarzy.

For a standard VAT invoice the block below works as a template unchanged:

<XmlExample copyable>
  ```xml
  <!-- ❌ Incomplete Adnotacje -->
  <Adnotacje>
    <P_16>2</P_16>
    <P_17>2</P_17>
  </Adnotacje>
  <!-- ✅ Complete Adnotacje (standard invoice) -->
  <Adnotacje>
    <P_16>2</P_16>
    <P_17>2</P_17>
    <P_18>2</P_18>
    <P_18A>2</P_18A>
    <Zwolnienie><P_19N>1</P_19N></Zwolnienie>
    <NoweSrodkiTransportu><P_22N>1</P_22N></NoweSrodkiTransportu>
    <P_23>2</P_23>
    <PMarzy><P_PMarzyN>1</P_PMarzyN></PMarzy>
  </Adnotacje>
  ```
</XmlExample>

## 12. P_6, P_6A and OkresFa

Three fields, three uses:

- **P_6** (in `Fa`): the delivery or service date, common to all lines,
- **P_6A** (in `FaWiersz`): the date for a specific line, when lines have different dates,
- **OkresFa** (in `Fa`): a billing period (for example a September subscription), with `P_6_Od` and `P_6_Do`.

In `Fa`, **P_6 and OkresFa are mutually exclusive** (`xs:choice` in the XSD). The ban on combining **P_6 with P_6A** is a separate semantic rule. `OkresFa` can appear together with `P_6A`, because it does not conflict with it at any level of the schema.

<XmlExample copyable>
  ```xml
  <!-- ❌ P_6 and P_6A at the same time -->
  <Fa>
    <P_6>2026-09-15</P_6>
    ...
  </Fa>
  <FaWiersz>
    <NrWierszaFa>1</NrWierszaFa>
    <P_6A>2026-09-14</P_6A>
    ...
  </FaWiersz>
  <!-- ✅ Option A: common delivery date (P_6 in Fa, no P_6A) -->
  <Fa>
    <P_6>2026-09-15</P_6>
    ...
  </Fa>
  <FaWiersz>
    <NrWierszaFa>1</NrWierszaFa>
    ...
  </FaWiersz>
  <!-- ✅ Option B: per-line dates (P_6A in FaWiersz, no P_6) -->
  <Fa>...</Fa>
  <FaWiersz>
    <NrWierszaFa>1</NrWierszaFa>
    <P_6A>2026-09-14</P_6A>
    ...
  </FaWiersz>
  <FaWiersz>
    <NrWierszaFa>2</NrWierszaFa>
    <P_6A>2026-09-15</P_6A>
    ...
  </FaWiersz>
  <!-- ✅ Option C: billing period (OkresFa instead of P_6) -->
  <Fa>
    <OkresFa>
      <P_6_Od>2026-09-01</P_6_Od>
      <P_6_Do>2026-09-30</P_6_Do>
    </OkresFa>
    ...
  </Fa>
  ```
</XmlExample>

`P_6` is not `P_1`. `P_1` is the invoice issue date and is always mandatory. `P_6` is the delivery or service date, often the same as `P_1` but with a different legal meaning.
