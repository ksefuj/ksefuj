On 6 November 2026 the Sejm passed the UD477 act, which moves the art. 106ni VAT Act fines to 1 January 2028. It is not law yet: the act still needs the Senate and the President, and until it appears in the Journal of Laws (Dziennik Ustaw) the 1 January 2027 date stands. **The act does not change the KSeF obligation.** The 10,000 PLN limit, which lets some 2026 invoices be issued outside KSeF, expires on 31 December 2026.

## Legal status as of 7 November 2026

<Info>
  The current act brings in art. 106ni from 1 January 2027. On 16 September 2026 the Ministry of
  Finance announced a deferral of fines to 31 December 2027 and a planned change to the act. On 20
  October the government sent draft UD477 to the Sejm (print no. 3245). On 6 November the Sejm
  passed the act: 437 MPs voted for, 1 against and 1 abstained. The Public Finance Committee
  (Komisja Finansów Publicznych) proposed no amendments, and the Sejm rejected the only amendments
  tabled at second reading, so the passed text is identical to the government bill. The act now goes
  to the Senate (planned sittings: 25-26 November, 8-9 December and 16-18 December). If the Senate
  amends or rejects it, the act returns to the Sejm. The President can then sign or veto it, and a
  signed act is published in the Journal of Laws. Older Ministry pages (FAQ KSeF 2.0, the Handbook)
  still say fines start on 1 January 2027. We update this paragraph when the act changes status.
</Info>

The act moves art. 106ni paras. 1-3 and 5-7 to 1 January 2028 and is itself to take effect on 31 December 2026, only one day before the current penalty date.

## What ends on 31 December 2026

In 2026 the art. 145m limit applied: up to 10,000 PLN gross of sales per month could be invoiced outside KSeF. At year end it expires together with art. 145n (cash register invoices and receipts with a NIP). From 2027 there are no cash register invoices, and a receipt with a NIP is no longer a simplified invoice. We covered the limit in [our article on the 10,000 PLN limit](/en/blog/ksef-10000-pln-limit).

The act passed by the Sejm does not change art. 145m or 145n. The Sejm rejected the amendments tabled by PiS (Law and Justice), with 168 for, 235 against and 40 abstaining. They would have let micro-entrepreneurs issue paper or electronic invoices outside KSeF, as well as cash register invoices, from 1 December 2026 to 31 December 2027. The amendment text set no 10,000 PLN threshold; it used the definition of a micro-entrepreneur in art. 7 para. 1 point 1 of the Entrepreneurs' Law (Prawo przedsiębiorców).

There are few permanent exceptions. Art. 106ga para. 2 of the VAT Act covers, among others, sellers with no seat or permanent establishment in Poland, invoices to private individuals not running a business, special procedures and the art. 113a exemption, and the Ministry of Finance regulation (Dz.U. 2025 item 1740, § 2) adds, among others, invoices for toll motorway travel and single tickets for passenger transport. The VAT exemption under art. 113 does not exclude you from KSeF.

## What is penalised and how much

Art. 106ni para. 1 lists three situations:

1. You did not issue a structured invoice in KSeF.
2. In emergency mode, during unavailability or in offline24 mode, you issued an invoice that does not match the template.
3. You did not send an invoice issued in offline24, emergency mode or during unavailability on time.

The deadlines behind point 3 are the next working day for offline24 and for unavailability (counted from its end), and 7 working days from the end of an outage in emergency mode.

The fine is up to 100% of the VAT shown on the invoice issued outside KSeF. If the invoice shows no VAT, it is up to 18.7% of the total amount due. The head of the tax office imposes it by decision, you pay within 14 days of delivery, and the money goes to the state budget.

For Ania it looks like this. A 12,300 PLN gross invoice with 2,300 PLN VAT issued outside KSeF has a ceiling of 2,300 PLN. A 500 PLN invoice from a VAT-exempt taxpayer has a ceiling of 93.50 PLN. That is a maximum, not a rate: the office applies the Code of Administrative Procedure rules on administrative fines, including art. 189d (factors for setting the amount), and where the violation is of negligible weight and has stopped it may issue a warning instead (art. 189f). These violations do not lead to fiscal crime or fiscal offence proceedings (para. 4).

## What changes if the act is published before 2027, and what if not?

|                                                        | Act published by 31.12.2026                 | No act by 31.12.2026                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| End of the 10,000 PLN limit and cash register invoices | 31.12.2026                                  | 31.12.2026                               |
| Fines under art. 106ni                                 | from 1.01.2028                              | from 1.01.2027                           |
| Para. 1 point 2                                        | without the reference to art. 106ne para. 3 | with the reference to art. 106ne para. 3 |

The last row concerns a total outage. Invoices from it are not entered into KSeF afterwards, so the explanatory memorandum to draft UD477 speaks of eliminating an "oczywisty błąd" (obvious error).

## No fine is not no consequences

The Ministry wrote plainly: "Odroczenie kar nie oznacza braku obowiązku stosowania KSeF" (deferring fines does not mean KSeF need not be used). In 2027 the National Revenue Administration is to support and remind first, but officials "będą reagować w przypadkach wystawiania faktur poza KSeF" (will react to invoices issued outside KSeF) and check whether not using the system leads to unreliable tax settlement.

The seller still owes the VAT on an invoice issued outside KSeF, and according to the Ministry the breach falls, as a rule, on the issuer.

The buyer is in a better position. In its "Najczęstsze pytania" (frequently asked questions) the Ministry states that the right to deduct remains, provided the art. 86 conditions are met and no art. 88 negative conditions apply. A missing KSeF invoice is there a "formal defect", and the buyer need not ask the seller to issue one. At the same time such an invoice is a "warning signal" and may raise the risk that the whole transaction is checked. The FAQ answer on deduction carries no time limit, but point 1.7 of the KSeF 2.0 Handbook part II is titled "Odliczenie podatku VAT z faktur wystawionych poza KSeF w okresie od lutego do grudnia 2026 r." (VAT deduction from invoices issued outside KSeF between February and December 2026). We found no Ministry answer on whether this position covers invoices from 2027, so the question is open. We cover the details in [VAT deduction from an invoice outside KSeF](/en/blog/invoice-outside-ksef-vat-deduction).

In practice: keep proof of the transaction (payments, protocols, correspondence) and note the suppliers who still invoice outside KSeF.

## What to do before 1 January 2027

Check that your invoicing software supports KSeF emergency and offline24 modes. The art. 106ni point 3 deadlines are short, so you need a procedure for when the system is unavailable. Offline mode needs a type 2 KSeF certificate obtained beforehand.

Tokens are allowed by the regulation until 31 December 2026 (§ 13). The KSeF 2.0 Handbook part I (06.08.2026, p. 26) says the Ministry "zdecydowało o zachowaniu tej metody uwierzytelniania na czas nieokreślony" (decided to keep this authentication method indefinitely) and that the regulation "zostanie odpowiednio znowelizowane" (will be amended accordingly). The amendment has not been published yet. So do not rely on a token alone; we explain the differences in [certificates vs tokens in KSeF](/en/blog/certificates-vs-tokens-ksef).

If you generate XML yourself, check it in the [ksefuj.to validator](/en/validator) before sending.
