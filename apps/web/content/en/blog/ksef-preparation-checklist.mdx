Before your first invoice in KSeF (Krajowy System e-Faktur, Poland's National e-Invoicing System), check the ten items below. Everyone who issues invoices, active VAT taxpayers and VAT-exempt ones alike, has been in the system since 1 April 2026. The exception, until the end of 2026, is sales up to 10,000 PLN a month. Not sure whether that applies to you? Start with our articles on [the deadlines](/en/blog/ksef-from-april-1-2026) and [the 10,000 PLN limit](/en/blog/ksef-10000-pln-limit).

_Legal position as of 7 October 2026._

## The checklist

### 1. Does my software support KSeF and FA(3)?

Since 1 February 2026 the only accepted format for structured invoices is FA(3), and the system rejects FA(2). Ask your provider directly: "Do you support KSeF 2.0 and FA(3)?" If the answer is "no" or "not yet", switch. Fakturownia, inFakt, wFirma and Comarch already support KSeF. We explain the schema differences in [FA(2) vs FA(3)](/en/blog/fa2-vs-fa3-ksef).

### 2. Can I log in to KSeF?

You can log in with **Profil Zaufany** (Poland's trusted identity profile, free, set up through online banking or mObywatel), the mObywatel app, your bank account, an e-ID card (via login.gov.pl), a qualified signature or seal, a KSeF certificate or a token. For most sole traders (JDG) Profil Zaufany is the simplest. Check that login works at [ap.ksef.mf.gov.pl](https://ap.ksef.mf.gov.pl/). We compare the methods in our article on [KSeF certificates and tokens](/en/blog/certificates-vs-tokens-ksef).

### 3. Have I checked a test invoice?

Create an invoice with fictitious data in your software, then drop the XML file into [ksefuj.to](/en/validator). The validator checks compliance with the FA(3) schema and MF requirements, and the file never leaves your browser.

<Tip>
  KSeF 2.0 has no built-in validator. You only find out about errors when the invoice is rejected,
  and a rejected invoice formally does not exist.
</Tip>

The most common errors are covered in our article on [FA(3) validation errors](/en/blog/most-common-fa3-validation-errors).

### 4. Have I sent an invoice to the MF test environment?

The test environment is at [ap-test.ksef.mf.gov.pl](https://ap-test.ksef.mf.gov.pl/web/). Invoices with fictitious data have no legal effect there, and you confirm that your software connects to KSeF correctly.

### 5. Do I know what to do when KSeF is down?

- **Offline mode** (no internet or a brief KSeF outage): issue the invoice locally and submit it to KSeF by **the end of the next business day**.
- **Emergency mode** (the MF announces a longer outage): issue invoices outside KSeF and submit them **within 7 business days** after the outage ends.

Your software should handle both modes automatically, so ask your provider. More in our [FAQ](/en/faq).

### 6. Does my accountant have access to KSeF?

An accounting firm needs permissions on your behalf. You grant them electronically in Aplikacja Podatnika KSeF 2.0 or through the API. The **ZAW-FA** form, filed at the tax office, is needed only if you are not a natural person and have no qualified seal, or if your signature or seal carries no NIP or PESEL.

<Warning>
  Without granted permissions your accountant cannot see your invoices in KSeF, even if they have
  kept your books for years.
</Warning>

### 7. Do I know the QR code on the invoice?

Invoices from KSeF carry a QR code that lets anyone verify the document. Your software generates it automatically. Just check that it appears on the printout.

### 8. Do I know where to get help?

The MF runs a helpdesk for KSeF questions, with contact details at [ksef.podatki.gov.pl](https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl). For tax questions you can call the Krajowa Informacja Skarbowa (National Tax Information) line.

### 9. Have I told my clients about the change?

Invoices go into KSeF instead of an inbox, and clients need their own KSeF access to retrieve purchase invoices. You can still send a PDF for convenience, but the legal document is the XML file registered in KSeF.

### 10. Do I have a plan B?

If your software fails, you have two options. [Aplikacja Podatnika KSeF](https://ap.ksef.mf.gov.pl/) is a free MF tool where you issue an invoice through a form (enough for 2–3 invoices a month). The other is the offline mode from point 5, which is a lawful mechanism, not a workaround.

## When penalties start

The KSeF obligation for everyone still starts on 1 January 2027. On 16 September 2026 the Ministry of Finance announced that it will extend the deferral of KSeF penalties until the end of 2027. A government draft amending the VAT Act (UD477, version of 22 September 2026, at the inter-ministerial consultation stage) would make Art. 106ni(1)–(3) and (5)–(7) apply from 1 January 2028. It is not law yet.
