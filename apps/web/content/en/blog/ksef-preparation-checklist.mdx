On 1 January 2027 two exceptions in the Polish VAT Act end: the 10,000 PLN gross monthly limit (Art. 145m) and invoices from cash registers (Art. 145n), so invoices to other businesses go through KSeF (Krajowy System e-Faktur, Poland's National e-Invoicing System) whatever the amount. Under the Act as it stands, the Art. 106ni penalties also start on 1 January 2027, but the Ministry of Finance (MF) has announced a deferral to the end of 2027, and draft UD477, which makes that change, is not law yet.

The KSeF obligation itself already applies to you: since 1 April 2026 (since 1 February 2026 if your 2024 sales exceeded 200 million PLN), including if you are VAT-exempt. Invoices to consumers still don't have to go into KSeF (Art. 106ga(2)(4)), and the same goes for the other permanent exclusions in Art. 106ga(2).

_Legal position as of 21 October 2026._

## What changes on 1 January 2027

### 1. Do you issue invoices outside KSeF under the 10,000 PLN limit?

Art. 145m covers only the period from 1 April to 31 December 2026. After that there is no threshold, including for VAT-exempt taxpayers (Art. 113). **What counts is the invoice issue date, not the sale date**, so an invoice for December 2026 issued in January 2027 already goes through KSeF. This follows from the wording of Art. 145m and the MF FAQ on the limit; MF has not answered this specific case separately.

If you send clients a PDF today, switch your software to KSeF submission before the end of the year. How to count the limit in the last months is covered in our article on [the end of the 10,000 PLN limit](/en/blog/end-of-10000-pln-limit). MF has not clarified whether invoices issued outside KSeF in 2026 must later be uploaded to the system.

### 2. Do you issue invoices from a cash register?

Until 31 December 2026 you can issue invoices using a cash register, and a receipt showing the buyer's NIP for up to 450 PLN (100 EUR) counts as a simplified invoice, both outside KSeF (Art. 145n(1)). From 1 January 2027 both options disappear. An invoice for a cash-register sale to another business is issued in KSeF.

You can issue an invoice to a business for a receipt only if the receipt shows the buyer's NIP (Art. 106b(5)). Breaking this rule carries a sanction of 100% of the tax amount (Art. 106b(6)). Details for shops and services are in our article on [cash-register invoices from 2027](/en/blog/cash-register-invoices-ksef-2027).

### 3. How does your software log in to KSeF?

The KSeF regulation allows tokens until 31 December 2026 (§13(1)(1), Journal of Laws 2025, item 1815). In the KSeF 2.0 Handbook, MF wrote that it has decided to keep this authentication method indefinitely and that the regulation will be amended accordingly. The amendment has not been published yet.

In September 2026 the KSeF API team said that tokens issued by 31 December 2026 will keep working, but they expire on 30 November 2027, and a new version of the token-generation service arrives on 1 January 2027. This is technical guidance, not law. If your software sends invoices to KSeF with a token, ask your provider whether it will move to the new tokens. More in our article on [KSeF tokens after 2026](/en/blog/ksef-tokens-after-2026).

### 4. Do you know when penalties start?

Under the Act in force, Art. 106ni(1)–(3) and (5)–(7) take effect on 1 January 2027. The penalty is up to 100% of the tax on the invoice or, when the invoice shows no tax, up to 18.7% of the total amount due. It covers, among other things, an invoice issued outside KSeF when it should have been in the system, and late submission of invoices issued in offline or emergency mode.

On 16 September 2026 MF announced that it would extend the penalty deferral to the end of 2027. In the same announcement it stressed that deferring penalties does not lift the obligation to use KSeF, and that the deferral requires a change to the Act. Draft UD477 contains that change: the version of 15 October 2026 has gone to the Council of Ministers and moves the penalties to 1 January 2028. **The draft has not been adopted or published.** If it is not published in the Journal of Laws (Dziennik Ustaw) before 31 December 2026, penalties apply from 1 January 2027. We track the current state in our article on [KSeF penalties in 2027 and 2028](/en/blog/ksef-penalties-2027-2028).

### 5. Do you pay invoices by bank transfer as an active VAT taxpayer?

For payments made on or after 1 January 2027, an active VAT taxpayer paying another active VAT taxpayer for a KSeF invoice puts its KSeF number in the transfer title (Art. 108g). When one transfer covers several invoices, you enter a collective identifier. The same applies to split payment transfers (Art. 108a(3)(3)). The payment date counts, not the invoice date, so a December invoice paid in January already needs the number. Invoices issued during a KSeF outage are not covered. How this works in practice is in our article on [the KSeF number in a bank transfer](/en/blog/ksef-number-in-bank-transfer).

### 6. For accountants: VAT deduction on invoices issued outside KSeF

MF's position on buyers deducting VAT from invoices issued outside KSeF covers the period from February to December 2026 (KSeF 2.0 Handbook, Part II, point 1.7). MF has not said how to treat such invoices issued in 2027.

## What to check even though it stays the same

**Software.** Ask your provider: "Do you support KSeF 2.0 and FA(3)?" For a few invoices a month, the free MF tools are enough: Aplikacja Podatnika KSeF (the KSeF Taxpayer App), Aplikacja Mobilna KSeF (the mobile app) and e-mikrofirma.

**Login.** Check that you can log in to [Aplikacja Podatnika KSeF](https://ap.ksef.mf.gov.pl/), for example with Profil Zaufany (Poland's free trusted profile).

**Permissions for your accountant.** Without them, your accounting firm cannot see your invoices in KSeF. You grant them electronically in Aplikacja Podatnika. The ZAW-FA form at the tax office is needed only by a business that is not a natural person and has no qualified seal, or when the signature or seal carries neither NIP nor PESEL (§4–5 of the regulation). Step by step: [KSeF access for your accountant](/en/guides/ksef-accountant-access).

**Outages and no internet.** The rules for offline and emergency mode do not change in 2027. What changes is that late submission of such invoices is subject to the Art. 106ni penalty. For offline invoices you need a type 2 KSeF certificate, and Aplikacja Podatnika issues online invoices only. The deadlines for each mode are in our guide to [KSeF outages and offline mode](/en/guides/ksef-outage-offline-mode).

**Test invoice.** KSeF 2.0 has no separate validator: you learn about an error only when the invoice is rejected, and a rejected invoice formally does not exist. You can test submission with no legal effect in the [MF test environment](https://ap-test.ksef.mf.gov.pl/web/).

If your software saves the invoice as an XML file, drop it into the [ksefuj.to validator](/en/validator) before your first invoice in January. The file never leaves your browser.
