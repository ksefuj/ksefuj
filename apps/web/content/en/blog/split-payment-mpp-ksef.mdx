On an FA(3) invoice in KSeF (Krajowy System e-Faktur, Poland's National e-Invoicing System), the split payment mechanism (MPP, _mechanizm podzielonej płatności_) is marked in field `P_18A` of the `Adnotacje` section: enter `1` when the total amount due exceeds PLN 15,000 (or its equivalent in a foreign currency) and the invoice covers goods or services listed in annex 15 to the VAT Act, and `2` in every other case. The field is mandatory on every invoice, including when MPP doesn't apply to you.

Legal position as of 22 October 2026.

## When to enter 1 and when 2

Both conditions must be met at the same time:

- **the total amount due exceeds PLN 15,000**; on a standard invoice that is the amount in field `P_15` (exactly PLN 15,000.00 does not exceed the threshold),
- the invoice covers a supply of goods or services listed in **annex 15** to the VAT Act.

If either is missing, you enter `2`. On advance, final settlement and corrective invoices, the schema gives `P_15` a different meaning (the amount paid, the amount still due, the correction amount), and MF sources don't say how to count the threshold in those cases. The schema annotation for the field also repeats the words "mechanizm podzielonej płatności" (split payment mechanism), and in KSeF their equivalent is the value `1`. <Source href="https://crd.gov.pl/wzor/2025/06/25/13775/schemat.xsd" label="FA(3) schema, element P_18A" />

An example: a wholesaler, a client of Pani Krystyna (an accountant), sells annex 15 goods on an invoice with a total amount due of PLN 18,450. `P_18A` = `1`. The same wholesaler issues an invoice for PLN 12,300 for the same goods: `P_18A` = `2`. Ania, a graphic designer, invoices PLN 20,000 for logo design. The amount is above the threshold, but if her service isn't in annex 15, she enters `2`.

We don't reproduce the full list of annex 15 items here. The FA(3) information sheet refers to its items when describing GTU codes: they include some electronics, waste and metals.

## Foreign currency invoices

The PLN 15,000 threshold also applies to invoices in euro or dollars. You convert the amount into złoty using the same rules you apply to the taxable amount (so says the annotation to `P_18A` in the schema). Those rules are in art. 31a of the VAT Act. As a rule it is the NBP (National Bank of Poland) average rate from the last business day before the tax point, but the provision has exceptions, including the option to use the ECB rate and a different date when the invoice is issued before the tax point.

An invoice for EUR 4,000 for annex 15 goods at an example rate of PLN 4.25 gives PLN 17,000. That is above the threshold, so `P_18A` = `1`. At an example rate of PLN 3.70 it would come to PLN 14,800, and `2`.

## Mandatory and voluntary MPP

As a rule, MPP is voluntary: the Act says that when paying, taxpayers "may apply" split payment (art. 108a ust. 1 of the VAT Act). It is mandatory, among other cases, for annex 15 goods and services when the invoice exceeds PLN 15,000 (art. 108a ust. 1a). For paying in breach of ust. 1a, the Act provides a sanction in art. 108a ust. 7. That sanction concerns how you pay, not whether the transfer carries a KSeF number. <Source href="https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/1263/text.pdf" label="VAT Act, art. 108a (Dz.U. 2026 item 1263)" />

`P_18A` describes only the mandatory case. The schema and the information sheet have no separate value for voluntary MPP.

## Line-level markers and GTU codes

Besides `P_18A`, the schema has two optional markers for individual items:

- `P_12_Zal_15` in an invoice line (`FaWiersz`): value `1` for goods or services from annex 15,
- `P_12Z_Zal_15` in an order line of an advance invoice (`ZamowienieWiersz`), with the same meaning.

You can leave out both, and neither replaces `P_18A`. The schema doesn't tie them to the value of `P_18A`. <Source href="https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/media/nuclnu0w/broszura-informacyjna-dotyczaca-struktury-logicznej-fa-3.pdf" label="FA(3) information sheet, §10.2 and §14.2" />

GTU codes are a separate matter. GTU_05, GTU_06 and GTU_08 cover, among others, annex 15 goods, but the information sheet describes GTU on the invoice as optional, and the schema refers to the JPK_VAT regulation for them. None of the sources we checked says that a GTU code forces `P_18A` = `1`. The value of `P_18A` depends on the threshold and annex 15.

## What the law and MF leave open

- **`1` below the threshold.** The schema says to enter `1` only when both conditions are met, and `2` "otherwise". The sources don't say whether a seller may enter `1` voluntarily, for example to encourage payment by MPP. Nor do they say whether a buyer may pay an invoice with `P_18A` = `2` by MPP.
- **Mixed invoices.** When only some items are from annex 15, MF materials don't tell us whether the threshold counts against the whole total amount due or only those items. The schema speaks of the invoice's "total amount due" where the invoice "covers" such goods.
- **Corrections.** We found no description of how to set `P_18A` on a corrective invoice, especially when the correction moves the amount across the PLN 15,000 threshold. The correction mechanism itself is covered in our post on the [corrective invoice in KSeF](/en/blog/corrective-invoice-ksef).

For a mixed invoice or a correction across the threshold, the answer depends on the specific transaction, so ask a tax adviser.

## MPP transfers and the KSeF number from 2027

For payments made from 1 January 2027, the MPP transfer message for a KSeF invoice carries its KSeF number instead of the invoice number (art. 108a ust. 3 pkt 3). The details, including the collective identifier for several invoices, are in our post on the [KSeF number in a bank transfer](/en/blog/ksef-number-in-bank-transfer).

## For developers: P_18A in XML

`P_18A` takes only `1` or `2` and sits in a fixed position: after `P_18`, before `Zwolnienie`. An `Adnotacje` fragment for an invoice subject to mandatory MPP:

<XmlExample copyable>
  ```xml
  <Adnotacje>
    <P_16>2</P_16>
    <P_17>2</P_17>
    <P_18>2</P_18>
    <P_18A>1</P_18A>
    <Zwolnienie>
      <P_19N>1</P_19N>
    </Zwolnienie>
    <NoweSrodkiTransportu>
      <P_22N>1</P_22N>
    </NoweSrodkiTransportu>
    <P_23>2</P_23>
    <PMarzy>
      <P_PMarzyN>1</P_PMarzyN>
    </PMarzy>
  </Adnotacje>
  ```
</XmlExample>

A missing `P_18A` is a validation error, not "no MPP". The other fields in this section and the usual mistakes with them are covered in [the most common FA(3) validation errors](/en/blog/most-common-fa3-validation-errors).

If you have an XML file and want to check that `Adnotacje` is complete, drop it into the [validator](/en/validator). It reports a missing `P_18A`, but doesn't check whether you picked the right value for the threshold and annex 15.
