You can issue an invoice to a private individual (a natural person not running a business) outside KSeF (Krajowy System e-Faktur, Poland's national e-invoicing system), because art. 106ga ust. 2 pkt 4 of the VAT Act excludes it from the mandate. If you want, you can issue it in KSeF voluntarily, but the customer won't find it in the system on their own: you hand it over in a way you agree on, with a QR code.

Legal status as of 8 October 2026.

## Outside KSeF: paper or PDF, as before

An invoice issued outside KSeF can be on paper or electronic. The electronic one doesn't need the FA(3) structure and doesn't go into the system: a PDF sent by email is enough.

Example: Kasia is a photographer running a JDG (jednoosobowa działalność gospodarcza, a Polish sole proprietorship). In October she does a family shoot for Marta, who asks for an invoice for 900 zł. Kasia issues it as a PDF in her invoicing software and emails it. She has nothing else to do.

If you use the VAT exemption (art. 113), you issue an invoice to a private individual only when they ask for one. The details, including the deadline for the request, are in our [post on KSeF for VAT-exempt businesses](/en/blog/vat-exempt-ksef).

## Issuing in KSeF: how the customer gets the invoice

A voluntary consumer invoice in KSeF does not reach the customer automatically. KSeF makes an invoice available to the buyer based on their NIP (Polish tax identification number), and a private individual has none on the invoice. That is why art. 106gb ust. 4 requires you to hand it over in a way agreed with the customer (by email, on paper), and an invoice handed over outside KSeF must carry a QR code. The Act doesn't prescribe the form of that agreement.

An invoice issued in KSeF in online mode has one QR code, with the KSeF number below it (an offline invoice, which has no number yet, has two codes). The customer uses it to download the invoice without logging in, in two steps:

1. They scan the code and see the basic data: KSeF number, seller's NIP, issue date. If the invoice is not in KSeF, they are told so.
2. They enter the invoice number, the information that the buyer has no identifier, and the total amount due. They can then download the invoice as XML or PDF.

Several failed attempts at the second step block access for a while. Without the QR code, the customer can also open the Aplikacja Podatnika (the MF's Taxpayer Application) without logging in and download the invoice, but then they also have to type in the KSeF number and their first and last name.

## The customer gives a NIP: now it's a business invoice

When the buyer has a NIP and buys for their business, the invoice falls under the KSeF mandate in art. 106ga ust. 1. Until 31 December 2026 you can still issue it outside the system if you stay within the 10,000 zł monthly limit, counted including VAT (art. 145m). Invoices to private individuals don't count towards that limit.

The NIP on an invoice in KSeF decides who the system makes it available to. A wrong NIP assigns the invoice to the business that number belongs to, so copy it carefully.

The MF materials we checked don't settle a few borderline cases:

- the customer runs a JDG but buys privately and still gives a NIP (or buys for the business and doesn't give one): who decides the nature of the purchase, and whether their declaration is enough;
- the customer runs unregistered activity (działalność nierejestrowana) or rents out property privately: whether they count as a person not running a business for this purchase;
- the customer is a flat-rate farmer (rolnik ryczałtowy).

If you often sell to such customers, agree on an approach with your accountant.

## Nothing changes here from 2027

The exclusion in art. 106ga ust. 2 pkt 4 has no end date. Two other exceptions apply until 31 December 2026: the 10,000 zł limit (art. 145m) and cash-register invoices (art. 145n). What happens when they expire is covered in our [post on the end of the 10,000 zł limit](/en/blog/end-of-10000-pln-limit). In 2027 you can keep issuing invoices to private individuals outside KSeF just as you do now.
