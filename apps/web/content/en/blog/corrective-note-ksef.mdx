According to Poland's Ministry of Finance (MF), corrective notes (nota korygująca) no longer apply from 1 February 2026, and an invoice sent to KSeF (Krajowy System e-Faktur, Poland's National e-Invoicing System) can't be edited, so the only way to fix it is a corrective invoice (faktura korygująca). If you're the buyer and spot a mistake in your name or address, in practice you report it to the seller, and the seller issues the correction. <Source href="https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/media/rronoxyt/podrecznik-ksef-20-cz-ii-wystawianie-i-otrzymywanie-faktur-w-ksef-06082026.pdf" label="KSeF 2.0 Handbook, part II, sections 1.6.2 and 6.3" />

A corrective note was the document a buyer issued itself to fix minor errors on an invoice it had received, such as a misspelt name or address. Legal position as of 16 October 2026.

## What MF says about corrective notes

The sentence about notes ending comes from MF's KSeF 2.0 Handbook, in the section explaining that an invoice sent to the system can't be edited. It is the ministry's position in its guidance materials. An invoice that has been assigned a KSeF number has entered legal circulation: it can't be cancelled or deleted, and KSeF stores it for 10 years from the end of the year it was issued. <Source href="https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/media/rronoxyt/podrecznik-ksef-20-cz-ii-wystawianie-i-otrzymywanie-faktur-w-ksef-06082026.pdf" label="KSeF 2.0 Handbook, part II, section 1.6.3" />

The buyer has no "reject" button in KSeF either. The system doesn't provide for accepting or rejecting invoices, and disputes between the parties are settled outside it. <Source href="https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/media/rronoxyt/podrecznik-ksef-20-cz-ii-wystawianie-i-otrzymywanie-faktur-w-ksef-06082026.pdf" label="KSeF 2.0 Handbook, part II, sections 6.1 and 6.3" /> How invoices reach the buyer is covered in our guide to [receiving invoices in KSeF](/en/guides/receiving-invoices-in-ksef).

## What the buyer does on spotting a mistake

Ania runs a JDG (jednoosobowa działalność gospodarcza, a Polish sole proprietorship) and ordered business cards from a print shop. On the invoice from KSeF her business is named "Ania Kowalska Desing" instead of "Design", and the address is missing the flat number. Before February 2026 her accountant, Pani Krystyna, would have advised issuing a corrective note. Now Ania emails the print shop with the correct details and asks for a corrective invoice.

The seller makes the changes: MF's instructions on invoice errors are addressed to the issuer, and a correction is an FA(3) file sent to KSeF by the seller (or by someone holding the seller's permissions). <Source href="https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/media/rronoxyt/podrecznik-ksef-20-cz-ii-wystawianie-i-otrzymywanie-faktur-w-ksef-06082026.pdf" label="KSeF 2.0 Handbook, part II, sections 1.6.2-1.6.4 and 2.13" />

If the invoice isn't yours at all, because it came from a company you don't work with, that's a different situation. What to do then is in our post on [fake invoices in KSeF](/en/blog/fake-invoices-ksef).

## What the seller issues

When the buyer's name or address is wrong, the seller issues a corrective invoice showing the buyer's details from before the correction alongside the correct ones. A wrong invoice number is also fixed with a correction. <Source href="https://crd.gov.pl/wzor/2025/06/25/13775/" label="FA(3) logical structure: Podmiot2K, NrFaKorygowany" />

**A wrong buyer NIP can't be corrected to a different one.** An invoice with someone else's NIP (Polish tax identification number) has already reached the company that holds that NIP, so the seller corrects it to zero and issues a new invoice with the right NIP. <Source href="https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/media/rronoxyt/podrecznik-ksef-20-cz-ii-wystawianie-i-otrzymywanie-faktur-w-ksef-06082026.pdf" label="KSeF 2.0 Handbook, part II, section 1.6.4" /> We walk through it in [zero-value correction when the buyer's NIP is wrong](/en/blog/corrective-invoice-ksef#zero-value-correction-when-the-buyers-nip-is-wrong).

The same rule applies to a test invoice sent to the production environment by mistake. Once accepted with a KSeF number it is a fully valid invoice and must be corrected to zero without delay. <Source href="https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/media/rronoxyt/podrecznik-ksef-20-cz-ii-wystawianie-i-otrzymywanie-faktur-w-ksef-06082026.pdf" label="KSeF 2.0 Handbook, part II, section 3.2.3" />

### FA(3) fields for accountants

The buyer's details from before the correction go in the `Podmiot2K` element (the full details from the invoice being corrected), and the correct ones in `Podmiot2`. The `IDNabywcy` field links the two. The seller has an equivalent, `Podmiot1K`, which can't be used to correct a NIP either. <Source href="https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/media/rronoxyt/podrecznik-ksef-20-cz-ii-wystawianie-i-otrzymywanie-faktur-w-ksef-06082026.pdf" label="KSeF 2.0 Handbook, part II, section 2.13.8" /> <Source href="https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/media/nuclnu0w/broszura-informacyjna-dotyczaca-struktury-logicznej-fa-3.pdf" label="FA(3) information sheet, §9.7" />

The correction cites the KSeF number of the invoice it corrects. If no number was assigned because that invoice was issued outside KSeF, `DaneFaKorygowanej` carries the flag `NrKSeFN` = `1` instead of the number. What decides this is whether the corrected invoice has a KSeF number, not its issue date. <Source href="https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/media/rronoxyt/podrecznik-ksef-20-cz-ii-wystawianie-i-otrzymywanie-faktur-w-ksef-06082026.pdf" label="KSeF 2.0 Handbook, part II, sections 2.13.6 and 4.1.3" />

## What we didn't find in MF's materials

The MF materials we work from don't answer three questions Pani Krystyna gets from clients:

- **Invoices issued lawfully outside KSeF.** Some invoices are still issued outside the system: until 31 December 2026, invoices within the [10,000 zł monthly limit](/en/blog/ksef-10000-pln-limit) and invoices from a cash register, as well as invoices for private individuals. MF's sentence about notes has no caveats, but it sits in the chapter on invoices sent to KSeF. We don't know whether a buyer can still issue a note for such invoices.
- **Notes issued before 1 February 2026.** These materials say nothing about notes issued before that date.
- **Self-billing.** MF covers self-billing in part III of the handbook, which we haven't analysed yet. We don't know whether it settles who corrects the invoice when the buyer issues invoices on the seller's behalf.

In these cases the answer depends on the specific invoice, so ask a tax adviser.

## A corrective note is not an accounting note

A nota obciążeniowa (debit note), nota uznaniowa (a note crediting the other party) and nota księgowa (accounting note) are different documents from a corrective note. According to MF, debit and crediting notes, like pro forma invoices, are not sent to KSeF. So the fact that you issue a debit note outside KSeF tells you nothing about corrective notes. <Source href="https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/media/rronoxyt/podrecznik-ksef-20-cz-ii-wystawianie-i-otrzymywanie-faktur-w-ksef-06082026.pdf" label="KSeF 2.0 Handbook, part II, section 1.1" />
