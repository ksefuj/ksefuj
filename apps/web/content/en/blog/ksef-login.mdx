You log in to KSeF (Krajowy System e-Faktur, Poland's National e-Invoicing System) with Profil
Zaufany or a qualified electronic signature: in the KSeF 2.0 Taxpayer Application, choose "Zaloguj
przez login.gov.pl" (Log in via login.gov.pl), then Profil Zaufany, mObywatel, online banking or
e-dowód. The second button, "Zaloguj certyfikatem" (Log in with a certificate), is for people and
companies with a qualified signature or seal.

Profil Zaufany (Trusted Profile) is the free Polish government e-ID; mObywatel is the government
mobile app and e-dowód is the Polish ID card with a chip. Legal status as of 7 October 2026.

## What the login looks like

The Taxpayer Application (Aplikacja Podatnika) runs at
[ap.ksef.mf.gov.pl](https://ap.ksef.mf.gov.pl). After you pick a login method, it asks on whose
behalf you want to act. You choose the identifier type (NIP, internal identifier, or a composite
identifier with an EU VAT number), enter it and click "Przejdź dalej" (Continue).

Ania is a freelance graphic designer running a JDG (jednoosobowa działalność gospodarcza, a Polish
sole trader business). She logs in with Profil Zaufany, chooses NIP and enters **her own NIP**. If
she also handled invoices for another company, she would enter that company's NIP at the same step.
That requires permissions in the context of that company.

## Sole traders: straight in, no ZAW-FA

If you run a sole trader business, KSeF assigns you owner permissions for your NIP automatically.
They cover issuing invoices, access to invoices, granting permissions to others (for example, your
accounting office), session history and viewing permissions. They cannot be revoked. You can log in
straight away with Profil Zaufany or a qualified signature.

**A sole trader does not file the ZAW-FA notification.** ZAW-FA is the form a taxpayer files with
the tax office to designate who may use KSeF. The only exception for a sole trader is registering
your own qualified signature when it contains neither a NIP nor a PESEL (the Polish personal ID
number); more on that below.

After logging in you will see invoices issued to your NIP. If one comes from a company you don't
know, check [how to spot a fake invoice in KSeF](/en/blog/fake-invoices-ksef).

## Companies: qualified seal or the person named in ZAW-FA

Tomasz is the president of a limited liability company (spółka z o.o.). His Profil Zaufany alone
will not open the company's invoices, because a company is not a natural person and gets no
permissions automatically. He has two routes:

- **The company's qualified seal.** Log in with the "Zaloguj certyfikatem" button.
- **A ZAW-FA notification.** In it the company names exactly one natural person, who does not have
  to be listed in the KRS (National Court Register). That person gets, among other things,
  permission management, invoice issuing and access to invoices, and adds further people
  electronically.

You can file ZAW-FA(3) on paper, through e-Urząd Skarbowy (Dokumenty → Złóż dokument, i.e.
Documents → Submit document) or through e-Doręczenia (the Polish e-delivery service). Filing through
ePUAP has not been effective since 1 January 2026. Permissions start working once the tax office
enters the data into the system, so don't leave it until the day you need to issue an invoice. Once
they work, the person named in ZAW-FA logs in with their own method (via login.gov.pl or a qualified
signature) and enters the company's NIP at the identifier step.

## Qualified signature without a NIP or PESEL

A qualified signature with a NIP or PESEL in the certificate works immediately. If the certificate
has neither, KSeF can't tell whose signature it is, and you have to register its **fingerprint** (a
SHA-256 hash, 64 hexadecimal characters). In the Taxpayer Application you use the "Pobierz odcisk
palca" (Get fingerprint) function: sign a file with the certificate and choose "Przekaż plik"
(Upload file).

Who registers the fingerprint depends on whose signature it is:

- **Your own, and you run a sole trader business:** you register it with a ZAW-FA notification (the
  exception from the sole trader section).
- **Someone you are granting permissions to:** you enter the fingerprint when granting permissions,
  with the identifier set to "Brak" (None), plus that person's date of birth and identity document.

After such a signature is renewed or replaced, the permissions have to be registered again. That
isn't needed if the new certificate already contains a NIP or PESEL.

### Sole trader businesses run by Ukrainian citizens

Olena, a Ukrainian citizen, runs a JDG in Poland. **The Taxpayer Application does not support login
with eIDs from other EU countries.** The manual does not list non-EU eIDs (such as Diia) among the
login methods, and we found no separate MF guidance for Ukrainian citizens. Olena therefore has the
same two routes as everyone else: login.gov.pl or a qualified signature. If her signature contains
neither a NIP nor a PESEL, she registers the fingerprint with a ZAW-FA notification, like any sole
trader. The KSeF documents do not settle whether a given person can set up Profil Zaufany or
mObywatel.

## KSeF token and certificate: for software, not for you

You cannot log in to the Taxpayer Application with a KSeF token or a KSeF certificate. They are for
invoicing software that connects to KSeF through the API. The software authenticates with a signed
request (XAdES) or a KSeF token. A KSeF certificate is generated after authenticating via
login.gov.pl or with a qualified signature or seal, and is valid for at most 2 years. A KSeF
certificate cannot be generated after authenticating with a token.

### What happens to tokens after 31 December 2026

This isn't settled. The regulation allows tokens to be used until 31 December 2026 (§ 13(1)(1)). In
its manual of 6 August 2026 the Ministry of Finance says tokens will stay "indefinitely" and the
regulation "will be amended accordingly". As of 7 October 2026 the amendment has not been
published. On 10 September 2026 the CIRF MF API team wrote on GitHub that tokens issued by 31
December 2026 will keep working, but no later than 30 November 2027. That is an announcement, not
law.

<Source
  href="https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/media/dmrfdixs/podrecznik-ksef-20-cz-i-rozpoczecie-korzystania-z-ksef-06082026.pdf"
  label="KSeF 2.0 Manual, Part I, p. 26"
/>

If your software connects to KSeF with a token, you don't need to do anything today. Ask your
software vendor how they are preparing for the end of 2026.

## Does Profil Zaufany still work in KSeF?

Yes, via login.gov.pl. The amending regulation (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 169) changed two dates: the
transitional legal basis for a standalone trusted signature (podpis zaufany) ended on 13 February
2026, and since 14 February 2026 there has been a permanent basis for logging in through the
national node, i.e. login.gov.pl, where Profil Zaufany is one of the methods. The KSeF 2.0 FAQ still
lists Profil Zaufany among the ways to log in to the Taxpayer Application.

It remains open whether KSeF still technically accepts a standalone trusted signature, for example
to sign a file in an API integration. The MF technical documentation still lists it, and the
Ministry has not settled the question.

<Source href="https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/169" label="Dz.U. 2026 poz. 169" />

## When Profil Zaufany doesn't work

The Ministry of Finance announces outages on its technical notices page. In 2026 these included
Profil Zaufany outages on 5 and 11 May and disruptions to the Taxpayer Application on 25 September.
Check there first to see whether the problem is on your side.

If only Profil Zaufany is unavailable, pick mObywatel, online banking or e-dowód on the same
login.gov.pl screen. Login with a qualified signature or seal goes through the separate "Zaloguj
certyfikatem" button. If the Taxpayer Application itself is down, switching login method won't help.

<Source href="https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/komunikaty-techniczne/" label="MF: technical notices" />
