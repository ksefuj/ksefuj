The regulation in force lets you authenticate to KSeF (Krajowy System e-Faktur, Poland's national e-invoicing system) with a token only until 31 December 2026. The Ministry of Finance (MF) and the KSeF API team say tokens will stay in use, but as of 7 October the regulation had not been changed.

## What exactly expires on 31 December

§ 13 ust. 1 pkt 1 of the regulation on using KSeF allows authentication with an "alphanumeric string" (a token) until the end of 2026. The permanent methods are listed in § 6 ust. 1: the national node of electronic identification, a qualified signature, a qualified seal and a KSeF certificate. Tokens are not on that list. <Source href="https://isap.sejm.gov.pl/isap.nsf/DocDetails.xsp?id=WDU20250001815" label="Dz.U. 2025 item 1815" />

Tokens from the old KSeF 1.0 are not compatible with KSeF 2.0, so the deadline concerns tokens generated for KSeF 2.0, in the Taxpayer Application (Aplikacja Podatnika) or through the API. <Source href="https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/pytania-i-odpowiedzi-ksef-20" label="MF FAQ, question 35" /> The provision points to a definition in the 2021 regulation and never says "KSeF 2.0 token". The KSeF 2.0 Handbook, however, treats these same tokens as covered by the 31 December 2026 date.

## What has been announced, and what does not exist yet

At the consultations on 9 June 2026 MF proposed keeping tokens after 31 December, with a validity period of 1 to 365 days and a new permission to renew a token automatically with the owner's consent. <Source href="https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/wyjasnienia/pierwsze-konsultacje-po-czesciowym-wdrozeniu-ksef-podsumowanie/" label="consultation summary" />

In the August edition of the KSeF 2.0 Handbook, part I (p. 26), MF went further: it "decided to keep this method of authentication for an indefinite period" and wrote that the regulation "will be amended accordingly". <Source href="https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/media/dmrfdixs/podrecznik-ksef-20-cz-i-rozpoczecie-korzystania-z-ksef-06082026.pdf" label="KSeF 2.0 Handbook, part I" />

On 10 September 2026 the team responsible for the KSeF API specification wrote in the official API repository that it is technically preparing to issue tokens under new rules from 1 January 2027. Tokens issued up to 31 December 2026 will still work for authentication, but their validity will be limited to 30 November 2027. The current token-creation endpoint is to run in "compatibility mode" and issue tokens with a limited lifetime, a new version of it will appear alongside, and the token format stays the same. <Source href="https://github.com/CIRFMF/ksef-api/issues/834#issuecomment-5619765888" label="CIRFMF/ksef-api, issue 834" /> The same author added that the decision to retire tokens is written into the regulation, so changing it requires a change of the rules, and that formal communication belongs to MF. <Source href="https://github.com/CIRFMF/ksef-api/issues/834#issuecomment-5003022923" label="comment of 17 July" />

On 23 September 2026 the same author closed another issue in the repository with: "tokens stay, but under new rules. We are refining the proposal." He did not say what those rules are. <Source href="https://github.com/CIRFMF/ksef-api/issues/565" label="CIRFMF/ksef-api, issue 565" /> The announced endpoint does not exist yet either: the API changelog up to version 2.9.0 (in production since 15 October 2026) mentions neither a new version of the token-creation endpoint nor token validity. <Source href="https://github.com/CIRFMF/ksef-api/blob/main/api-changelog.md" label="API changelog" />

All of this is announcements, not law: the law is § 13 as it stands. We do not know:

- what the amendment will say and when it takes effect,
- what rules will cover tokens issued from 2027 (MF proposed validity of 1 to 365 days and renewal with the owner's consent), and whether MF or the regulation will confirm the 30 November 2027 limit for tokens issued by the end of 2026. Today a token works until revoked,
- what the system will do on 1 January 2027 if the amendment is late. With § 13 as it stands, tokens are not permitted by the regulation from that date.

## What to do now

**Accounting offices.** Pani Krystyna should check which of her clients' programs log in to KSeF with a token. The Taxpayer Application lists generated tokens under "Lista tokenów". <Source href="https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/media/qocjn3ai/podrecznik-uzytkownika-aplikacji-podatnika-ksef-20_06082026.pdf" label="Taxpayer Application manual, ch. 5.6" /> It is also worth asking the software vendor whether it supports KSeF certificates and what it plans after 31 December. A token generated by a natural person (for example a sole trader) may be used only by that person, so an office cannot use a token generated by a client. <Source href="https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/media/dmrfdixs/podrecznik-ksef-20-cz-i-rozpoczecie-korzystania-z-ksef-06082026.pdf" label="KSeF 2.0 Handbook, part I, p. 38" />

### For integrators

Marek, whose integration authenticates with a token, should not wait for the amendment. We suggest adding authentication with a type 1 KSeF certificate next to the token and hiding both behind one interface. Then the 1 January date does not force a rewrite. The API team's stated goal is to get through 2026/2027 "without touching the integrator's code", but it also says tokens will get an expiry date, so it makes sense to prepare renewal handling.

| Feature                       | Token                    | KSeF certificate (type 1)    |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| Permissions                   | stored in the token      | none, taken from the account |
| Context                       | the one it was issued in | several                      |
| Validity                      | today until revoked      | up to 2 years                |
| Interactive and batch session | yes                      | yes                          |

Renew a certificate before it expires, and note that the number of active certificates per PESEL and NIP is limited. You request it in the API or the Taxpayer Application after logging in with the national node, a qualified signature or a qualified seal. A token login cannot generate a certificate (§ 13 ust. 2), so you need one of those three.

<Info>
  A type 2 certificate marks invoices issued OFFLINE with a QR code and is not used for logging in.
  For authentication you need type 1, issued separately.
</Info>
