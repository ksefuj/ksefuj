The KSeF mobile app (Aplikacja Mobilna KSeF) is a free tool from Poland's Ministry of Finance (MF) for issuing and receiving invoices in KSeF (Krajowy System e-Faktur, Poland's National e-Invoicing System). It issues only basic domestic invoices in PLN, though, and it corrects only invoices issued in the same app (KSeF Mobile App Manual, section 1.1, edition of 31 August 2026).

Status as of 9 November 2026.

## What can you do in the mobile app?

The MF lists the mobile app among its free tools, alongside the KSeF Taxpayer App (Aplikacja Podatnika KSeF) and e-mikrofirma. The KSeF 2.0 Manual describes it briefly: it is for issuing and receiving invoices in real time and managing them from anywhere.

Ewa is a translator running a JDG (jednoosobowa działalność gospodarcza, a Polish sole proprietorship). Each month she issues two invoices in PLN to Polish translation agencies. She can issue both in the mobile app, and if she gets an amount wrong, she can correct them there too. If she takes a job from a German agency billed in euros, she can't issue that invoice in the mobile app.

## When do you need the Taxpayer App or invoicing software?

The mobile app is not enough when the invoice is:

- in a foreign currency;
- for a cross-border transaction;
- a type other than a basic invoice, for example an advance invoice;
- a correction of an invoice issued outside the mobile app, in the Taxpayer App or other software.

The MF materials we checked contain no complete list of invoice types the app doesn't support. In these cases, use the web-based KSeF Taxpayer App (ap.ksef.mf.gov.pl), which is also free, or invoicing software integrated with KSeF.

## Does the mobile app work offline?

The MF documents we checked don't say. Press reports about issuing invoices offline in the mobile app in 2027 aren't confirmed in the ministry materials we checked. About the Taxpayer App, the MF says plainly that it issues "only invoices in online mode".

If you want a plan for a KSeF outage, you need software that supports offline mode and a KSeF certificate prepared in advance. Our [guide to outages and offline mode](/en/guides/ksef-outage-offline-mode) explains how to set this up.

## How do you log in to the mobile app?

The MF materials we rely on don't describe logging in to the mobile app or where to download it. For current information, see the [KSeF mobile app page on the MF website](https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/aplikacja-podatnika-ksef-i-inne-narzedzia/aplikacja-mobilna-ksef/). The ways to log in to the Taxpayer App are covered in our [KSeF login guide](/en/guides/ksef-login).

As a natural person with a NIP (Polish tax identification number) who is a VAT taxpayer, you get owner permissions in KSeF automatically, without filing the ZAW-FA form. You don't create an account and you pay nothing.

## Can you receive purchase invoices in it?

Yes, the MF lists the mobile app as a tool for receiving invoices. As a business with a NIP, since 1 February 2026 you receive in KSeF the invoices that sellers issued in KSeF, and if you have no software integrated with KSeF, the free MF tools are enough for this. The MF materials we checked don't show what the list of purchase invoices looks like in the app itself.

## What changes from 1 January 2027?

That's when the 10,000 PLN limit under Art. 145m of the VAT Act ends. Until 31 December 2026, it lets you issue invoices outside KSeF as long as your monthly sales on them don't exceed 10,000 PLN gross. According to the KSeF 2.0 Manual, the free MF tools may serve as your main invoicing tool from 2027.
