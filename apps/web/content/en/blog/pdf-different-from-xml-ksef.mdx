When you issue an invoice in KSeF (Krajowy System e-Faktur, Poland's National e-Invoicing System), the invoice is the FA(3) XML file the system assigned a KSeF number to, and the PDF you email to your customer is a visualisation of it, which the Ministry of Finance (MF) says must be consistent with the XML and must not contradict it. The MF materials we reviewed do not say whether a PDF that differs from the XML is a "pusta faktura" (an "empty invoice", the Polish term for an invoice that documents no real transaction), which document prevails when they disagree, or what tax consequences such a mismatch has.

Legal status as of 15 October 2026.

## What is the invoice and what is the visualisation

The KSeF Manual defines a structured invoice as an invoice issued using KSeF, together with its assigned number, in XML format. The KSeF number itself is not part of the file: the FA(3) structure has no field for it, and the system returns it in the UPO (the official receipt confirmation). That is why you see the number and the QR code on the printout but not in the XML.

MF describes an invoice handed over outside the system (for example a PDF by email, or a printout) as an invoice "issued in the agreed manner or used outside KSeF". There are three requirements: consistency with the XML file, legibility, and no contradiction with the XML content. The visualisation may carry extra elements with no meaning for the system, such as a logo or customer service contact details, and may even be in a different language from the XML. What counts is that the substance matches.

A domestic buyer whose NIP (Polish tax identification number) is entered in the NIP field receives the invoice in KSeF, and the date of receipt is the day the KSeF number is assigned (Art. 106na(3) of the VAT Act). Sellers covered by the obligation do not issue invoices to such buyers outside the system. Once the KSeF number is assigned, they may send them a visualisation of the invoice with a QR code; before that, only a transaction confirmation, which MF says is not a tax document. Our guide to [receiving invoices in KSeF](/en/guides/receiving-invoices-in-ksef) explains how the buyer finds the invoice.

## What MF hasn't settled

Pani Krystyna keeps the books for a dozen or so companies. A client sends her a PDF for PLN 1,230.00, while KSeF holds an XML for PLN 1,320.00 under the same invoice number. In the MF materials we reviewed, we found no answer to the questions that come up at that point:

- whether the mismatched PDF is an "empty invoice" or a second invoice,
- which document prevails when the content differs,
- whether the buyer may deduct VAT based on the amounts shown on the PDF,
- whether an inconsistent visualisation carries a penalty.

MF says one thing directly: an invoice accepted with a KSeF number is a fully valid invoice and creates an obligation to pay the tax (Art. 108 of the VAT Act), and the error must be fixed without delay with a correction "to zero". That passage of the Manual concerns test invoices sent to production by mistake. That the same applies to other errors in the XML is our conclusion, not MF's position. If the mismatch involves amounts you have already booked or deducted, talk to a tax adviser.

## When the answer is different

### Buyer without a NIP or from abroad

A consumer, an entity without a NIP, a foreign company and an EU taxpayer using the SME scheme (Art. 106gb(4)) receive the invoice in the manner agreed with the seller, for example as a PDF; the Act does not prescribe the form. Such an invoice must carry a QR code, and the date of receipt is the day the buyer actually received it (Art. 106na(4)). The consistency requirement covers it too, because the Manual section explicitly refers to an invoice "issued in the agreed manner". Details in our post on [invoices for private individuals](/en/blog/invoice-for-private-person-ksef).

### Invoice issued offline

An offline invoice that does not yet have a KSeF number carries two QR codes: "OFFLINE" and "CERTYFIKAT". Until the invoice has a KSeF number, a buyer with a NIP may receive only a transaction confirmation, and gets the invoice itself in KSeF. The exception is emergency mode (Art. 106nf): then the invoice with both QR codes goes to the buyer outside KSeF. MF calls handing an offline24 invoice to the buyer outside KSeF before it has been submitted "incorrect conduct by the seller"; the buyer then acquires the right to deduct on the date they actually received the document. Our guide to [KSeF outages and offline mode](/en/guides/ksef-outage-offline-mode) covers offline mode in more detail.

  label="KSeF 2.0 Manual, Part II, sections 1.6.5, 1.6.6 and 1.8"
/>

### Seller not yet in KSeF

Until 31 December 2026, a seller within the PLN 10,000 limit (Art. 145m) or issuing invoices from a cash register (Art. 145n) may issue an invoice outside KSeF. In that case the invoice (paper, electronic such as a PDF, or a cash register printout) is an ordinary invoice rather than a visualisation, and there is no XML to compare it with.

## How to fix a mismatch

An invoice with a KSeF number cannot be edited, cancelled or deleted, and correction notes (noty korygujące) have not been available since 1 February 2026. **If the error is in the XML, the seller issues a corrective invoice**, stating the amounts as the difference from the original invoice. A new PDF with corrected amounts does not replace it. If the wrong buyer was named, it takes a correction "to zero" and a new invoice. Our post on the [corrective invoice in KSeF](/en/blog/corrective-invoice-ksef) walks through it step by step.

The buyer cannot reject an incorrect invoice in KSeF, because the system has no such option, and disputes are settled outside it. For wrong amounts, the buyer can ask the seller for a correction. KSeF does not check the arithmetic either, so an XML with a wrong VAT amount gets a number just like a correct one.

If the XML is correct and only the PDF was wrong, MF does not say what to do. Our advice: send the buyer a new visualisation generated from the XML stored in KSeF and tell them the previous file was wrong.

## How to check whether a PDF matches KSeF

The QR code on the invoice (KOD I) contains, among other things, the issue date, the seller's NIP and a SHA-256 hash of the XML file. Scanning it shows the invoice's basic data from KSeF, or a message that no such invoice exists in the system. In the second step you enter the invoice number (P_2), the buyer's tax identifier and the total amount due (P_15), and download the XML or PDF without logging in. Several failed attempts trigger a temporary block. Without a QR code, you can open the invoice anonymously in the Aplikacja Podatnika (MF's taxpayer app): you need the KSeF number, P_2, the buyer's identifier and name, and P_15.

<Source
  href="https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/media/rronoxyt/podrecznik-ksef-20-cz-ii-wystawianie-i-otrzymywanie-faktur-w-ksef-06082026.pdf"
  label="KSeF 2.0 Manual, Part II, sections 5.2, 5.4.1, 5.4.3, 5.5 and 5.7"
/>

We draw two conclusions from what the code contains (ours, not MF's). A code generated from a different file than the one in KSeF should not lead to the invoice. **The code contains no amounts, so on its own it cannot compare what the PDF shows with the XML.** Take care in the second step: the system asks for P_15 from the invoice in KSeF, so an amount copied from a wrong PDF may not work, and repeated attempts will lock you out. If you have a NIP, it is simpler to log in to KSeF, open the invoice there and compare it with the PDF field by field.

## For developers: one XML, one source for the PDF

Marek maintains an ERP in which one module builds the XML for KSeF, while an old template prints the PDF by reading data straight from the database. Any change in one place without a matching change in the other produces a mismatch. It is safer to generate the visualisation from the same XML file that received the KSeF number, and to add the KSeF number and QR code only once the UPO arrives.

MF does not recommend sending KSeF an XML with only the basic data while keeping the payment due date or bank account number on the visualisation alone. That data has a place in the FA(3) structure: `Platnosc`, `Rozliczenie`, `WarunkiTransakcji`, `DodatkowyOpis`. Annotations go on the visualisation only when they apply (for example `P_16` = 1 means "metoda kasowa", cash accounting); you don't need to describe annotations that are absent. MF also publishes its own visualisation generator on GitHub, but we found no requirement in MF materials to use it.
