## What this error means

One of the amounts on the invoice contains a space, a comma or some other character that isn't a digit. In FA(3) an amount is written in digits only, with a dot before the decimal part: `1234.56`, not `1 234,56`.

## Why it's required

The Ministry of Finance's FA(3) information brochure (broszura) requires amount fields to be a string of digits with no thousands separators (no spaces), and the dot to be the only permitted decimal separator. A negative amount takes a minus sign in front. The XSD schema defines these fields as numbers, so a value with a space or a comma also fails schema validation.

Our validator reports this error when a field contains anything other than digits, a dot and a minus sign, or has more than one dot. We check:

- in the `Fa` summary: `P_13_x` (including `P_13_6_1`–`P_13_6_3`), `P_14_x`, `P_14_xW`, `P_15` and `KursWalutyZ`;
- in every `FaWiersz` line: `P_8B` (quantity), `P_9A` and `P_9B` (unit prices), `P_10` (discount), `P_11`, `P_11A`, `P_11Vat` and `KursWaluty`.

The error details show the field name and, for lines, the line number too.

## How to fix it

1. Remove every space from the amount, including the one between thousands.
2. Replace a decimal comma with a dot.
3. Remove the currency, percent signs and any other additions. You give the currency once, in `KodWaluty`.
4. If the amount uses a dot as the thousands separator (`1.234,56`), remove it before replacing the comma, so you end up with `1234.56`. The same goes for English-style `1,234.56`: drop the comma.

<XmlExample copyable>
  ```xml
  <!-- ❌ Space as thousands separator -->
  <P_15>4 920.00</P_15>
  <!-- ❌ Decimal comma -->
  <P_15>4920,00</P_15>
  <!-- ❌ Dot for thousands and decimal comma -->
  <P_15>4.920,00</P_15>
  <!-- ❌ Currency in the amount field -->
  <P_15>4920.00 zł</P_15>
  <!-- ✅ Digits only, decimal dot -->
  <P_15>4920.00</P_15>
  <!-- ✅ Negative amount, e.g. in a correction line -->
  <P_11>-150.00</P_11>
  ```
</XmlExample>

## Where it usually comes from

Number formatting is usually to blame. A spreadsheet or program with Polish regional settings displays amounts as `4 920,00`, and the export writes the display-formatted text into the XML instead of the raw number. Sometimes it's a non-breaking space you can't see in the editor. The other source is amounts copied by hand from a PDF or a printout.

The number of decimal places is a separate rule: amounts have at most 2, quantities 6, unit prices 8. It's covered in our [overview of the most common FA(3) errors](/en/blog/most-common-fa3-validation-errors#6-too-many-decimal-places). Once you've fixed it, [check the invoice again](/en/validator).
