## What this error means

The VAT rate field (`P_12`) in one of the invoice lines has a value that isn't on the FA(3) list. The field accepts only predefined codes, such as `23`, `0 KR` or `zw`, written exactly as in the catalogue and without a percent sign.

## Why it's required

In its description of `P_12` in `FaWiersz`, the Ministry of Finance's FA(3) information brochure (broszura) gives a closed catalogue of values, and the XSD schema accepts only those. Letter case and spaces matter: `0 KR` is a different value from `0KR`, and `zw` from `ZW`.

| Value      | Meaning                                                                                |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `23`, `22` | standard rate                                                                          |
| `8`, `7`   | first reduced rate                                                                     |
| `5`        | second reduced rate                                                                    |
| `4`, `3`   | 4% and 3% rates                                                                        |
| `0 KR`     | 0% domestic, other than intra-EU supply (WDT) and export                               |
| `0 WDT`    | 0% on an intra-Community supply of goods (WDT)                                         |
| `0 EX`     | 0% on an export of goods                                                               |
| `zw`       | VAT-exempt sale                                                                        |
| `oo`       | domestic reverse charge                                                                |
| `np I`     | supply outside Poland, not taxable in Poland (excluding Art. 100 ust. 1 pkt 4 and OSS) |
| `np II`    | services under Art. 100 ust. 1 pkt 4 of the VAT Act                                    |

Our validator compares `P_12` in every line against this list character by character and shows the value it found in the message. An empty or omitted `P_12` doesn't trigger this error. The brochure allows the rate to be left out under the margin scheme, for exempt sales referred to in Art. 106b ust. 3 pkt 2 of the VAT Act, and on a simplified invoice.

## How to fix it

1. Check the message for the line and value the validator flagged.
2. Pick a code from the table. For a 0% rate, always give the variant: `0 KR`, `0 WDT` or `0 EX`.
3. Remove the `%` sign, any decimals, and leading or trailing spaces.

<XmlExample copyable>
  ```xml
  <!-- ❌ Percent sign -->
  <P_12>23%</P_12>
  <!-- ❌ Number with decimals -->
  <P_12>23.00</P_12>
  <!-- ❌ Bare 0, no variant -->
  <P_12>0</P_12>
  <!-- ❌ Uppercase -->
  <P_12>ZW</P_12>
  <!-- ❌ Missing space -->
  <P_12>npI</P_12>
  <!-- ✅ Valid codes -->
  <P_12>23</P_12>
  <P_12>0 KR</P_12>
  <P_12>zw</P_12>
  <P_12>np I</P_12>
  ```
</XmlExample>

<Warning>
  `oo` applies only to domestic reverse charge. On an invoice for a foreign buyer who accounts for
  VAT in their own country, the brochure points to `np I` or `np II`. The validator checks this with
  a separate rule.
</Warning>

## Where it usually comes from

The program or spreadsheet stores the rate as a number or as display text (`23%`, `23.00`, `ZW`) and exports it to XML without mapping it to an FA(3) code. The other source is a 0% rate written as a bare `0`, with no indication of whether it's a domestic sale, WDT or export.

How to choose the rate for customers in other countries is explained in our post on [invoicing a foreign buyer](/en/blog/foreign-invoice-ksef).
