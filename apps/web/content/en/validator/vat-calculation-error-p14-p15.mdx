## What this error means

The amounts in the invoice summary don't agree with each other: either the tax doesn't follow from the net amount and the rate, or the total due isn't the sum of net and VAT. KSeF (Krajowy System e-Faktur, Poland's national e-invoicing system) will accept the invoice anyway, because it doesn't check arithmetic. A wrong VAT amount still has to be fixed afterwards with a corrective invoice.

## What exactly we check

This rule isn't in the Ministry of Finance's FA(3) information brochure (broszura). It's an extra check of ours, built on what the brochure says the fields mean: `P_13_x` is net sales at a given rate, `P_14_x` is the tax on them, and `P_15` is the total amount due. The validator:

- calculates `P_14_1` from `P_13_1` at 23% or 22%, `P_14_2` from `P_13_2` at 8% or 7%, and `P_14_3` from `P_13_3` at 5%, rounded to the grosz; matching either rate in a group is enough;
- also accepts tax calculated per line and summed for each rate;
- compares `P_15` with the sum of all net amounts (`P_13_x`, including `P_13_6_1`–`P_13_6_3` for 0% rates) and tax amounts (`P_14_x`) in the summary; tax converted to PLN (`P_14_xW`) is not part of that sum;
- allows a difference of up to 0.01 PLN with either calculation method;
- skips corrective invoices (`KOR`, `KOR_ZAL`, `KOR_ROZ`), because they contain differences rather than full amounts.

A rate outside the catalogue in `P_12` triggers a separate message, covered on the page about an [invalid VAT rate](/en/validator/invalid-vat-rate-p12).

## How to fix it

1. For each rate, multiply the net amount by the rate and round to the grosz. Ania invoices 4,000.00 PLN net at 23%: the tax is 920.00 PLN.
2. Put the sum of all `P_13_x` and `P_14_x` fields in `P_15`. For Ania: 4,000.00 + 920.00 = 4,920.00 PLN.
3. If the invoice has several rates, calculate each one separately and add everything up in `P_15`.

<XmlExample copyable>
  ```xml
  <!-- ❌ Net amount entered in P_15 instead of gross -->
  <P_13_1>4000.00</P_13_1>
  <P_14_1>920.00</P_14_1>
  <P_15>4000.00</P_15>
  <!-- ❌ Tax calculated on the gross amount -->
  <P_13_1>4000.00</P_13_1>
  <P_14_1>1131.60</P_14_1>
  <P_15>5131.60</P_15>
  <!-- ✅ 4000.00 × 23% = 920.00; 4000.00 + 920.00 = 4920.00 -->
  <P_13_1>4000.00</P_13_1>
  <P_14_1>920.00</P_14_1>
  <P_15>4920.00</P_15>
  <!-- ✅ Two rates: 100 + 23 + 200 + 16 = 339 -->
  <P_13_1>100.00</P_13_1>
  <P_14_1>23.00</P_14_1>
  <P_13_2>200.00</P_13_2>
  <P_14_2>16.00</P_14_2>
  <P_15>339.00</P_15>
  ```
</XmlExample>

## Where it usually comes from

- **Tax calculated on the gross amount.** 4,920.00 × 23% gives 1,131.60 instead of 920.00.
- **P_15 filled with the net amount**, or with the amount before a discount.
- **Line-level tax doesn't match the summary.** Art. 106e ust. 10 of the Polish VAT Act allows tax to be calculated per line and the results summed, so the validator accepts a `P_14_x` equal to the sum of rounded line taxes at that rate (`P_11` × rate) or to the sum of the `P_11Vat` fields (FA(3) brochure, description of `P_11Vat`). The message appears when `P_14_x` matches none of these calculations, nor the tax on the whole base, for example after someone edits an amount in the summary by hand.

A shorter description with a different example is in our [overview of the most common FA(3) errors](/en/blog/most-common-fa3-validation-errors#10-p15-doesnt-match-the-totals). Once you've fixed it, [check the invoice again](/en/validator).
