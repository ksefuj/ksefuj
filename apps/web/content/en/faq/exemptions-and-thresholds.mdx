## Thresholds and Exemptions

<Question q="What is the 10,000 PLN threshold?">
  Taxpayers with monthly invoiced sales not exceeding 10,000 PLN can issue invoices outside KSeF
  until December 31, 2026. This applies to any taxpayer, including VAT-exempt ones (Art. 145m of the
  VAT Act). From January 1, 2027 there is no limit. Check the current regulations at
  ksef.podatki.gov.pl or consult your accountant.
</Question>

<Question q="How is the 10,000 PLN threshold calculated?">
  The threshold is calculated as the total gross (including VAT) value of invoices subject to the
  KSeF obligation issued in a given calendar month, not total revenue or the number of invoices.
  Both VAT-taxable and VAT-exempt B2B invoices subject to KSeF count. Sales through cash registers,
  B2C invoices (for private individuals), and simplified invoices (receipts with NIP up to 450 PLN)
  do not count. If you are approaching the threshold or are unsure how the rules apply to you,
  consult your accountant: the regulations in this area have been amended.
</Question>

<Question q="When does KSeF apply to me if I'm below the threshold?">
  The KSeF obligation covers you from April 1, 2026, whether you are an active VAT taxpayer or
  VAT-exempt (from February 1, 2026 it covered only businesses whose 2024 sales exceeded 200 million
  PLN). Until December 31, 2026, however, you can issue invoices outside KSeF as long as their total
  gross value in a given month does not exceed 10,000 PLN (Art. 145m of the VAT Act). The invoice
  that takes you over the threshold, and every invoice after it, must be issued in KSeF, even if
  your sales drop below 10,000 PLN the following month (Art. 145m ust. 2). From January 1, 2027
  there is no threshold.
</Question>

<Question q="Does a flat-rate farmer need to use KSeF?">
  The VAT Act does not list flat-rate farmers (rolnik ryczałtowy) among the KSeF exclusions (Art.
  106ga ust. 2). What matters is whether you issue an invoice. As a flat-rate farmer supplying
  agricultural products, you are exempt from the duty to issue invoices (Art. 117 of the VAT Act),
  so you can document a sale with a rachunek (a simple bill) instead of an invoice (Podręcznik KSeF
  2.0, cz. II). A VAT RR invoice is issued by the buyer, an active VAT taxpayer who buys
  agricultural products from you (Art. 116). [From April 1, 2026](/en/blog/ksef-from-april-1-2026)
  the buyer can issue it in KSeF voluntarily, if you submit a statement in KSeF that you are a
  flat-rate farmer and name that buyer as authorised. Without that statement, the VAT RR invoice is
  issued outside KSeF. If you issue invoices as a taxpayer, for example from another business
  activity, KSeF covers them under the general rules. Check with your accountant whether you keep
  flat-rate farmer status in that case.
</Question>

<Question q="Who is exempt from KSeF?">
  The VAT Act (Art. 106ga ust. 2) excludes from KSeF, among others, invoices to private individuals
  not running a business (you can issue them in KSeF voluntarily), invoices issued by taxpayers with
  no registered office or fixed establishment in Poland, invoices for transactions settled under the
  non-Union scheme, the import scheme (IOSS) and the special scheme for occasional international
  road passenger transport, and invoices issued by taxpayers from another EU country using the
  exemption in Art. 113a. Until December 31, 2026 you can also issue cash-register invoices outside
  KSeF (Art. 145n). Further cases are set out in the regulation issued under Art. 106s; if in doubt,
  check ksef.podatki.gov.pl.
</Question>

<Question q="Does a foundation or association need to use KSeF?">
  Yes, if it issues invoices. The legal form does not matter: a foundation or association that
  issues invoices as a VAT taxpayer, active or exempt, does so in KSeF from April 1, 2026 under the
  same rules as other taxpayers, with the same exclusions and the 10,000 PLN threshold until the end
  of 2026. An organisation that does not issue invoices is not subject to the obligation to issue
  them in KSeF.
</Question>
