## Thresholds and Exemptions

<Question q="What is the 10,000 PLN threshold?">
  Taxpayers with monthly invoiced sales not exceeding 10,000 PLN can issue invoices outside KSeF
  until December 31, 2026. This applies to any taxpayer, including VAT-exempt ones (Art. 145m of the
  VAT Act). From January 1, 2027 there is no limit. Check the current regulations at
  ksef.podatki.gov.pl or consult your accountant.
</Question>

<Question q="How is the 10,000 PLN threshold calculated?">
  The threshold is calculated as the total gross (including VAT) value of invoices subject to the
  KSeF obligation issued in a given calendar month, not total revenue or the number of invoices.
  Both VAT-taxable and VAT-exempt B2B invoices subject to KSeF count. Sales through cash registers,
  B2C invoices (for private individuals), and simplified invoices (receipts with NIP up to 450 PLN)
  do not count. If you are approaching the threshold or are unsure how the rules apply to you,
  consult your accountant: the regulations in this area have been amended.
</Question>

<Question q="When does KSeF apply to me if I'm below the threshold?">
  The date from which KSeF applies to smaller taxpayers depends on VAT status and current
  regulations: the rules have been amended several times. Always verify the applicable dates
  directly at ksef.podatki.gov.pl or consult your accountant.
</Question>

<Question q="Does a flat-rate farmer need to use KSeF?">
  The VAT Act does not list flat-rate farmers (rolnik ryczałtowy) among the KSeF exclusions (Art.
  106ga ust. 2). What matters is whether you issue an invoice. If, as a flat-rate farmer, you are
  exempt from the duty to invoice (Art. 117 of the VAT Act), you can document a sale with a rachunek
  (a simple bill) instead of an invoice (Podręcznik KSeF 2.0, cz. II). VAT RR invoices, issued when
  buying farm produce from a flat-rate farmer, can be issued in KSeF voluntarily [from April 1,
  2026](/en/blog/ksef-from-april-1-2026), and issuing them outside KSeF is still allowed. If you
  issue invoices as a taxpayer, for example from another business activity, KSeF covers them under
  the general rules. Check with your accountant whether you keep flat-rate farmer status in that
  case.
</Question>

<Question q="Who is exempt from KSeF?">
  Exemptions include taxpayers without a registered office or fixed place of business in Poland,
  invoices issued under special procedures (OSS/IOSS), and invoices from cash registers (until
  December 31, 2026, Art. 145n). The full list of exemptions is defined in the Polish VAT Act and
  secondary legislation; check ksef.podatki.gov.pl for details.
</Question>

<Question q="Does a foundation or association need to use KSeF?">
  It depends on whether the organization is registered as an active VAT taxpayer. If so, from April
  1, 2026 it must use KSeF for its VAT invoices. Organizations conducting exclusively VAT-exempt
  activities are subject to KSeF on the same deadlines as other taxpayers. Organizations that do not
  issue VAT invoices at all are not subject to the obligation.
</Question>
