## KSeF Basics

<Question q="What is KSeF?">
  KSeF (Krajowy System e-Faktur) is the Polish Ministry of Finance platform for issuing and
  receiving structured invoices. Every B2B invoice must be submitted to KSeF as an XML file and
  receive an official number before it reaches the buyer. The system acts as a central tax invoice
  register maintained by the state.
</Question>

<Question q="When did KSeF become mandatory?">
  For large businesses (sales above 200M PLN in 2024), KSeF became mandatory on February 1, 2026.
  For all other VAT taxpayers, active and VAT-exempt alike, it has applied since April 1, 2026.
  Until December 31, 2026 some invoices can still be issued outside KSeF: when monthly sales stay
  within 10,000 PLN (Art. 145m of the VAT Act) and when the invoice is issued with a cash register
  (Art. 145n). From January 1, 2027 these exceptions end.
</Question>

<Question q="Who does KSeF apply to?">
  KSeF applies to VAT taxpayers, active and VAT-exempt, who have a registered office or fixed place
  of business in Poland and issue invoices (Art. 106ga of the VAT Act). It covers invoices to
  businesses and institutions (B2B). Invoices to consumers, meaning individuals not running a
  business, are excluded (Art. 106ga ust. 2 pkt 4). VAT-exempt taxpayers follow the same deadlines
  as everyone else.
</Question>

<Question q="Does KSeF apply to consumer invoices (B2C)?">
  No. Invoices to consumers, meaning individuals not running a business, are outside the KSeF
  obligation (Art. 106ga ust. 2 pkt 4 of the VAT Act), whether you issue them from a cash register
  or in invoicing software. You can issue them in KSeF voluntarily (Art. 106ga ust. 4), but you
  still deliver the invoice to the customer in the way you agreed, with a QR code (Art. 106gb ust.
  4). If the customer buys for their business and gives you a NIP, it is a B2B invoice.
</Question>

<Question q="Does KSeF apply to till receipts with a NIP number?">
  Until December 31, 2026, a till receipt showing the buyer's NIP for up to 450 PLN (or EUR 100)
  counts as a simplified invoice and can be issued outside KSeF. Until that date you can also issue
  invoices with a cash register outside KSeF (Art. 145n of the VAT Act). From January 1, 2027 a
  receipt with a NIP is no longer a simplified invoice, and an invoice to a business for a
  cash-register sale is issued in KSeF (Podręcznik KSeF 2.0, cz. I).
</Question>

<Question q="What is FA(3)?">
  FA(3) is the current XML schema for structured invoices, published by the Polish Ministry of
  Finance. The number 3 indicates the third version of the format. All invoices submitted to KSeF
  must comply with this schema — any deviation results in rejection by the system.
</Question>

<Question q="What is the difference between FA(2) and FA(3)?">
  FA(2) was the previous schema version, used in KSeF 1.0 (until 31 January 2026). FA(3) introduced
  a new XML namespace, updated fields, and expanded annotation requirements. FA(2) invoices are not
  accepted by KSeF 2.0 — if your software was generating FA(2), confirm with your vendor that an
  update to FA(3) has been released.
</Question>

<Question q="Can I still send PDF invoices?">
  No — PDF is not accepted by KSeF as a tax document. The system requires a structured XML file
  compliant with FA(3). You can still send the buyer a PDF visualization for convenience, but the
  legal version of the document is the XML file registered in KSeF. Good invoicing software
  generates both: XML for KSeF and PDF for the buyer.
</Question>

<Question q="What is a KSeF number?">
  A KSeF number is a unique invoice identifier assigned by the MF system once the invoice is
  registered. You need it when correcting an invoice issued in KSeF: the correction gives the KSeF
  number of the original invoice (the NrKSeF and NrKSeFFaKorygowanej fields). If the original
  invoice has no KSeF number because it was issued outside KSeF, you tick the NrKSeFN field instead.
</Question>

<Question q="What is UPO?">
  UPO (Urzędowe Poświadczenie Odbioru — Official Receipt Confirmation) is a document confirming that
  an invoice was accepted by KSeF. It contains the KSeF number, acceptance date, and a digital
  signature from the system. UPO proves that the invoice was correctly registered — it is worth
  archiving alongside the invoice XML file.
</Question>
