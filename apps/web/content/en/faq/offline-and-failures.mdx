## Offline Mode and Failures

<Question q="What should I do if KSeF is down?">
  You can issue the invoice outside KSeF in offline mode. If MF announces that KSeF is unavailable
  or declares an outage, separate rules and deadlines apply for sending those invoices to KSeF. In a
  total outage, invoices are not sent to KSeF at all (Art. 106ng of the VAT Act).
</Question>

<Question q="What is offline mode?">
  In offline mode (offline24) you issue the invoice outside KSeF and send it to KSeF no later than
  the next business day after the issue date (Art. 106nda of the VAT Act). The law does not tie this
  mode to an outage, so you can use it at any time. If you hand an offline invoice to the buyer
  outside KSeF, it carries two QR codes, and you need a type 2 KSeF certificate to generate the
  second one. The Taxpayer Application issues only online invoices, so offline mode requires
  software that supports it.
</Question>

<Question q="What is emergency mode?">
  Emergency mode is officially declared by the Ministry of Finance when KSeF is unavailable due to
  technical or administrative reasons. During this time the obligation to send invoices to KSeF is
  suspended — you issue invoices outside the system and commit to uploading them after the outage.
  Status updates appear on ksef.podatki.gov.pl.
</Question>

<Question q="How long do I have to submit invoices after an outage?">
  You send an invoice issued in emergency mode to KSeF within 7 business days of the day the outage
  ended, as stated in the MF announcement (Art. 106nf ust. 4 of the VAT Act). If MF announces
  another outage during that time, the period starts again. An invoice issued while KSeF was
  unavailable goes to KSeF no later than the next business day after the unavailability ends (Art.
  106nh ust. 2), and an offline24 invoice no later than the next business day after its issue date
  (Art. 106nda ust. 2). Invoices issued during a total outage are not sent to KSeF (Art. 106ng).
</Question>

<Question q="What QR codes does an offline invoice carry?">
  An offline invoice that does not yet have a KSeF number carries two QR codes. The first, labelled
  "OFFLINE", links to the invoice in KSeF and lets the reader check its data. The second, labelled
  "CERTYFIKAT", confirms the issuer's identity. Your invoicing software generates both codes, and
  the second one requires a type 2 KSeF certificate that you must obtain in advance (Podręcznik KSeF
  2.0, cz. II, pkt 5.4).
</Question>

<Question q="How do I know if KSeF is unavailable?">
  MF publishes outage announcements in the Public Information Bulletin (BIP) and in the KSeF
  interface software, unavailability announcements in the Bulletin, and total outage announcements
  in the mass media (Art. 106ne of the VAT Act). Your invoicing software should also display a
  message when it cannot connect to the MF API.
</Question>
