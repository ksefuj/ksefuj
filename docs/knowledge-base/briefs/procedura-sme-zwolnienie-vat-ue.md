# Research Brief: Procedura SME (zwolnienie z VAT w innych państwach UE) a KSeF

**Requested by:** coordinator (six-new-posts batch) **Date:** 2026-10-26 **For content:** Blog post
`procedura-sme-zwolnienie-vat-ue`

**Target query:** „procedura SME VAT”, „zwolnienie z VAT w innym kraju UE”, „numer EX VAT”

**Persona:** Ania, JDG on the domestic art. 113 exemption, sells digital products or services to
consumers in other EU countries.

**Answer in two sentences:** Since 1 Jan 2025 a business seated in Poland whose EU-wide annual
turnover does not exceed 100 000 euro (previous and current year) may be VAT-exempt in another
member state on that state's conditions, after filing an advance notification (SME-P) in e-Urząd
Skarbowy to the Naczelnik Drugiego Urzędu Skarbowego Warszawa-Śródmieście and receiving the number
PL[NIP]-EX; it then files a quarterly information (SME-IK) within a month after each quarter (VAT
Act art. 113b; podatki.gov.pl SME pages). KSeF's permanent exclusion (art. 106ga ust. 2 pkt 6)
concerns the opposite direction (foreign SME exempt in Poland under art. 113a); a Polish SME's
domestic invoices for businesses still go to KSeF.

## Key Facts (ready to use)

### Legal basis and numbering

1. **Articles are 113a and 113b only** (no 113c–113m). Art. 113a = foreign EU SME exempt in Poland;
   art. 113b = Polish SME exempt in other member states. S1, p. 157–160. HIGH
2. **Implementing act:** ustawa z 8 listopada 2024 r. o zmianie ustawy o VAT, Dz.U. 2024 poz. 1721;
   directive 2020/285 (Dz. Urz. UE L 62 z 2.03.2020) and 2022/542. In force from 1 Jan 2025. S3.
   HIGH (as stated by MF)
3. **Data scope regulation:** rozporządzenie MF z 22 grudnia 2024 r., Dz.U. 2024 poz. 1969 (content
   of advance notification and quarterly information). S4, S6 Q31. HIGH (not fetched itself)

### Polish SME exempt abroad (art. 113b)

4. **Conditions:** seat in Poland and „roczny obrót na terytorium Unii Europejskiej nie przekroczył
   w poprzednim ani bieżącym roku podatkowym kwoty 100 000 euro”; exemption „na warunkach
   określonych przez to państwo członkowskie”. Art. 113b ust. 1, S1 p. 158. HIGH
5. **Definition of EU turnover** (art. 113a ust. 1 pkt 1, applied via 113b): total annual value of
   supplies whose place of taxation is the EU, excl. VAT, including Polish sales. S1 p. 157. FAQ
   Q11: Polish sales and turnover in states where SME isn't used count too. FAQ Q40: reverse-charge
   services taxed in another state also count (directive art. 288). FAQ Q15: goods and services.
   HIGH
6. **National threshold per state:** each state sets its own; MF: „nie może on jednak przekroczyć 85
   000 euro”; may differ by sector; states may allow continuation after exceeding (e.g. +10% / +25%)
   but never above 100 000 euro in that state; grace period for re-entry 1 or 2 years; exclusions
   for specific supplies. S4 („Pamiętaj!” box). HIGH (MF guidance; directive 2020/285 art. 284
   confirms 85 000 / 100 000 EUR, checked 2026-10-26)
7. **Which states:** obliged for states that have a domestic exemption (directive art. 284 ust. 2 w
   zw. z ust. 1, per S6 Q1). S9: „na dzień 1 stycznia 2025 r. Hiszpania nie przewiduje zwolnienia z
   VAT w ramach procedury szczególnej SME”; SME does not apply to supplies with place of supply in
   Northern Ireland. National rules: EC page (S6 Q2). MEDIUM (current list not verified)
8. **Optional:** taxpayer may use SME in some states and general rules / OSS in others (S3, S6 Q10,
   Q12, Q41). SME-P does not deregister from OSS (Q42). An existing foreign VAT registration is not
   lost automatically (Q17). HIGH
9. **VAT-czynny in Poland may also use SME abroad** (S6 Q13, S9). Domestic art. 113 status is not a
   condition. HIGH
10. **VAT-R first:** taxpayers exempt under art. 113 / 43 not yet registered as „podatnik VAT
    zwolniony” must file VAT-R before the advance notification. Art. 96 ust. 3c, S1 p. 106; S4; FAQ
    Q8 (VAT-R in e-US), Q37 (działalność nierejestrowana needs NIP and VAT-R). HIGH
11. **Competent office:** Naczelnik Drugiego Urzędu Skarbowego Warszawa-Śródmieście. Art. 3 ust. 3
    pkt 2 lit. e, S1 p. 11; S3 („niezależnie od tego, gdzie podatnik rozlicza się z tytułu innych
    obowiązków w VAT”). HIGH
12. **Advance notification** („uprzednie powiadomienie”): taxpayer data, chosen states, EU turnover
    in previous and current year (art. 113b ust. 2–3). S4: split per state incl. Poland; some states
    require two years back. Form **SME-P** in e-US („Deklaracje” → „Złóż e-Deklarację” → kafel
    „Procedura SME”), S7. HIGH
13. **Only via e-Urząd Skarbowy:** art. 113b ust. 18; FAQ Q38. Account needs a current e-mail or
    phone (ust. 19, S7). NIP required (S7). HIGH
14. **Decision in 35 working days** after receipt (art. 113b ust. 4; FAQ Q36: counted from the next
    day, excluding Saturdays, Sundays, holidays); may be extended for anti-evasion verification.
    HIGH
15. **Number format:** „PL[NIP]-EX”, same number in every state (S6 Q14, S8). Art. 113b ust. 4 pkt
    1: „polski numer identyfikacyjny EX”. Verification on EC „SME on the Web”. HIGH
16. **Exemption starts** on the day the taxpayer is informed of the EX number being granted or
    confirmed; until then, old rules (S6 Q31, S4; art. 113a ust. 4 mirrors it for the inbound case).
    Note S6 Q18: no exemption abroad until the EX number arrives. HIGH
17. **Refusal:** EU turnover > 100 000 euro → postanowienie o odmowie nadania numeru, zażalenie
    (ust. 5). Not meeting a state's conditions → information on refusal for that state; if all
    states refuse → postanowienie (ust. 6). FAQ Q43: zażalenie within 7 days (Ordynacja art. 236).
    HIGH
18. **Updates** (art. 113b ust. 7): another state, resignation from a state, change of data. New
    state: 35 working days (ust. 8). HIGH
19. **Resignation effective** (ust. 10): from the first month of the next quarter if notified in the
    1st or 2nd month of a quarter; from the second month of the next quarter if notified in the last
    month. Resigning in all states → number deactivated (ust. 11). FAQ Q23: can resign in one state
    only. HIGH
20. **Quarterly information SME-IK** (art. 113b ust. 14–15): within a month of the quarter's end
    (deadline runs even when the last day is Saturday or a holiday); content: EX number, turnover in
    each member state for the quarter incl. Polish sales; correction „niezwłocznie”. Values in euro
    (S4). FAQ Q24: zero returns required; Q25: also during suspension of business; Q35: corrections
    only by correcting the given quarter, not in the next one; Q39: full values, no rounding. HIGH
21. **Catch-up returns** (ust. 16): if EX granted in a later quarter, file SME-IK for all quarters
    from the notification onwards with the first one. HIGH
22. **Exceeding 100 000 euro:** SME-IK for the period from the start of the quarter to the day of
    exceeding, within 15 working days (ust. 17); number deactivated „niezwłocznie”, postanowienie
    immediately enforceable (ust. 12 pkt 1). Other deactivation triggers: conditions lost /
    exemption ceased in all states, end of business (ust. 12 pkt 2–4). HIGH
23. **Currency:** ECB rate published on the first day of the year (or next publication day), art.
    113b ust. 21 → art. 113a ust. 8. HIGH
24. **No input VAT deduction** for supplies abroad exempt under art. 113b: art. 86 ust. 8 pkt 1
    excludes them (S1 p. 87). FAQ Q32: no deduction for Polish purchases tied to the exempt foreign
    activity. HIGH
25. **Turnover rules abroad:** use each state's rules (FAQ Q16, Q21). HIGH 25a. **Proxies:** an
    agent files SME forms with PPO-1 or PPS-1EU; to sign SME-IK also UPL-1 (FAQ Q26); an existing
    UPL-1 suffices (Q4). HIGH

### When Ania doesn't need SME at all

26. **42 000 zł rule for B2C cross-border e-services and intra-EU distance sales of goods**: below
    10 000 euro (42 000 zł for a Polish supplier), summed across the EU, in neither the current nor
    the previous year, place of taxation stays in Poland (art. 22a ust. 1 pkt 3 and art. 28k ust. 2
    pkt 3, S1). FAQ Q18 confirms: SME doesn't change place-of-supply rules; above 42 000 zł (or by
    election) the supply is taxed in the consumer's state, where SME may then be used. HIGH
27. Art. 113 ust. 2 pkt 1: intra-EU distance sales not taxed in Poland are excluded from the Polish
    240 000 zł limit. S1 p. 156. HIGH

### Domestic exemption (contrast)

28. **Art. 113 ust. 1: 240 000 zł** (previous and current year), domestic sellers only. S1 p. 156.
    HIGH

### Foreign SME exempt in Poland (art. 113a), briefly

29. Seat in another member state; EU turnover ≤ 100 000 euro; Polish sales ≤ the art. 113 ust. 1
    amount; no art. 113 ust. 13 supplies. Notification and EX number in the home state (ust. 2).
    Competent Polish office: Naczelnik Urzędu Skarbowego Łódź-Śródmieście (art. 3 ust. 3 pkt 4).
    Generally no VAT-R (art. 96 ust. 3d). Exemption lost from the transaction exceeding either
    amount (ust. 7). Must keep daily sales records (art. 109 ust. 1). Polish invoice to such a buyer
    must show the buyer's EX number (art. 106e ust. 1 pkt 27; FAQ Q9); invoice by such a seller
    shows its own EX number (pkt 25). HIGH
30. **Contradiction:** S5 (dated 27.02.2026) still says „200 000 zł” for the Polish sales cap of
    art. 113a; the law refers to the art. 113 ust. 1 amount, which is 240 000 zł from 1 Jan 2026 (S1
    p. 156, footnote 29; transitional art. 2 ust. 3 of the amending act applies it to 113a
    taxpayers). Use 240 000 zł; flag that the MF page is stale.

### KSeF

31. **Art. 106ga ust. 2 pkt 6:** KSeF obligation does not apply to invoices „przez podatnika
    korzystającego ze zwolnienia, o którym mowa w art. 113a ust. 1” (foreign SME exempt in Poland).
    Ust. 3: such invoices are electronic or paper. S1 p. 126. HIGH
32. **Art. 106gb ust. 4 pkt 4:** a structured invoice to a buyer using art. 113a is made available
    to the buyer „w sposób z nim uzgodniony”. So a Polish seller invoicing a foreign SME still
    issues it in KSeF and delivers it outside KSeF. S1 p. 127. HIGH
33. **Polish SME (113b) gets no KSeF exclusion.** Art. 106ga ust. 2 lists no 113b case. Its domestic
    invoices for businesses go to KSeF as for any art. 113 seller (temporary art. 145m 10 000 zł
    rule until 31 Dec 2026; consumer invoices excluded, pkt 4). HIGH
34. **Supplies abroad under 113b are outside Polish invoicing chapter:** art. 106a (scope of
    „Rozdział 1 Faktury”, which contains art. 106ga) covers „sprzedaż” (taxable in Poland) and, for
    supplies with place of taxation in another member state, only those where the buyer owes the VAT
    (reverse charge, pkt 2 lit. a). A B2C supply taxed in another state therefore falls outside the
    Polish invoicing rules and outside the KSeF obligation. S1 p. 121–122. Confidence: MEDIUM
    (reading of art. 106a; MF has not said it in the SME pages or KSeF FAQ). Which documents the
    foreign state requires: not researched; check that state's rules. For B2C the KSeF conclusion
    holds anyway via art. 106ga ust. 2 pkt 4 (HIGH). Caveat: art. 106b ust. 1 pkt 2 separately lists
    WSTO (invoice unless OSS); its interplay with 106a for SME-exempt WSTO is unclear. Directive
    2020/285 (PL text, publications.europa.eu cellar 95e6b981…, checked 2026-10-26) adds art. 220a
    ust. 1 lit. c: states may allow simplified invoices for art. 284 SMEs.
35. **Art. 106b ust. 2:** no invoice obligation for sales exempt under art. 113 ust. 1 and 9 or 113a
    ust. 1 (on request: ust. 3). Does not mention 113b. HIGH

## Warning: Common Misconceptions

- „Zwolnieni z art. 113a nie wystawiają faktur w KSeF” is sometimes read as covering Polish SMEs
  using the exemption abroad. It does not: pkt 6 is about foreign sellers exempt in Poland.
- SME does not replace OSS automatically and does not change place-of-supply rules.
