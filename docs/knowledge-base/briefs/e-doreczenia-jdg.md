# Research Brief: e-Doręczenia (adres do doręczeń elektronicznych) dla JDG

**Requested by:** coordinator (content batch `content/six-new-posts`) **Date:** 2026-10-26 **For
content:** blog post `apps/web/content/pl/blog/e-doreczenia-jdg.mdx`

**Target query:** „e-doręczenia jdg termin” / „nie mam adresu do e-doręczeń co grozi”

**Answer in two sentences:** A JDG registered in CEIDG by 31.12.2024 had to obtain an entry of its
electronic delivery address (ADE) in the baza adresów elektronicznych (BAE) by 30.09.2026 (art. 152
ust. 3 ustawy o doręczeniach elektronicznych); a JDG registering from 1.01.2025 provides the data in
the CEIDG registration application (art. 152 ust. 1). The act contains no fine for missing the
deadline; the practical consequence is that public bodies send letters to the ADE once it exists
(with a 14-day deemed-delivery rule), and without an ADE they send paper via the public hybrid
service (art. 4-5; Ordynacja art. 144 § 1a-1b).

**Scope note:** the topic is outside the usual MF corpus. Tier 1 here = ustawa z 18.11.2020 o
doręczeniach elektronicznych (t.j. Dz.U. 2026 poz. 3) and Ordynacja podatkowa (t.j. Dz.U. 2026 poz.
622). Tier 3 = gov.pl/e-doreczenia and biznes.gov.pl service pages. All fetched live 2026-10-26. No
commercial or news sources used.

**What changes the answer:**

- Registration date in CEIDG (by 31.12.2024 vs from 1.01.2025) and whether the JDG filed a CEIDG
  change between 1.07.2025 and 30.09.2026 (then the ADE data was required in that change, art. 152
  ust. 2).
- KRS entities have different dates (fact 4).
- Whether the taxpayer acts through a pełnomocnik (Ordynacja art. 145 § 2: letters go to the
  pełnomocnik).
- Pending: Dz.U. 2026 poz. 507 (in force 2026-10-14) amended art. 152 ust. 1-2 wording only (CEIDG
  application names), not the deadlines.

## Key Facts (ready to use)

1. **Obligation for CEIDG entrepreneurs.** Art. 9 ust. 1 pkt 9: a podmiot niepubliczny entered in
   CEIDG must have an ADE entered in BAE, linked to the public registered e-delivery service (PURDE)
   or a qualified service. In force from 1.10.2026 (art. 166 pkt 9). Source: Dz.U. 2026 poz. 3, art.
   9 ust. 1 pkt 9, art. 166 pkt 9. Tier 1. HIGH. Ania: yes.
2. **Deadline for JDG registered by 31.12.2024.** Art. 152 ust. 3: those without an ADE in BAE,
   „wpisane do Centralnej Ewidencji i Informacji o Działalności Gospodarczej” (other than ust. 1-2)
   „są obowiązane uzyskać wpis adresu do doręczeń elektronicznych do bazy adresów elektronicznych w
   terminie do dnia 30 września 2026 r.” Tier 1. HIGH. Ania: yes. gov.pl (16.09.2026, 20.10.2026)
   and biznes.gov.pl phrase it as „od 1 października 2026 r. … zobowiązani do posiadania aktywnego
   adresu”. Consistent.
3. **Registered from 1.01.2025 / changes from 1.07.2025.** Art. 152 ust. 1: those filing a CEIDG
   registration from 1.01.2025 must provide data to create a PURDE address or a qualified-service
   ADE. Art. 152 ust. 2: those registered by 31.12.2024 without an ADE who file a CEIDG change
   between 1.07.2025 and 30.09.2026 must provide the data (or apply to the minister). Tier 1. HIGH.
4. **KRS entities.** Art. 9 ust. 1 pkt 8 applies from the date in the komunikat under art. 155 ust.
   10 (art. 166 pkt 5), set at 1.01.2025 (Komunikat MC z 29.05.2023, Dz.U. 2023 poz. 1077, as
   amended by Dz.U. 2024 poz. 1078). Art. 151 ust. 1: KRS entities registered before that date had 3
   months, i.e. until 1.04.2025 (biznes.gov.pl: „od 1 kwietnia 2025 roku”). New KRS entities from
   1.01.2025 at registration. Tier 1 + 3. HIGH. Ania: no.
5. **No penalty in the act.** The consolidated text contains no fine or sanction for not having an
   ADE (searched for „kar”, „grzywn”, „sankcj”: no hits). Tier 1. HIGH (absence). Do not invent
   penalties. Other statutes not checked.
6. **What public bodies do without an ADE in BAE.** Art. 4 ust. 1: a public body delivers
   correspondence needing proof of sending/receipt to the ADE in BAE. Art. 4 ust. 2: if not in BAE,
   to the ADE from which the non-public entity sent its correspondence. Art. 5 pkt 1: if delivery to
   ADE is impossible, via the public hybrid service (PUH: electronic document turned into a paper
   letter by the designated operator, delivered as a registered letter with electronic proof of
   receipt, art. 45 ust. 2 pkt 6, art. 46 ust. 1; not an ordinary letter). Art. 6: exceptions (paper
   originals, technical reasons, etc.). Tier 1. HIGH.
7. **Tax office delivery rules.** Ordynacja podatkowa art. 144 § 1a: „Organ podatkowy doręcza pisma
   na adres do doręczeń elektronicznych, chyba że doręczenie następuje na konto w systemie
   teleinformatycznym organu podatkowego albo w siedzibie organu podatkowego.” § 1b: if not
   possible, by the designated operator via PUH or staff; § 1c: then registered post. § 1d:
   decisions with rygor natychmiastowej wykonalności may go by § 1c. Art. 144a § 1: to the ADE in
   BAE, or the qualified-service ADE used to file, if none in BAE; § 2: delivery date per art. 42
   ustawy o doręczeniach. Source: Dz.U. 2026 poz. 622. Tier 1. HIGH. Ania: yes.
8. **Pełnomocnik receives tax letters.** Ordynacja art. 145 § 1-2: letters are served on the party,
   or if a pełnomocnik is appointed, on the pełnomocnik at the address in the power of attorney.
   Tier
   1. HIGH. Practical meaning for e-Doręczenia boxes not stated by the act.
9. **Deemed delivery after 14 days.** Art. 41 ust. 1 pkt 3, art. 42 ust. 2: correspondence from a
   public body not picked up is deemed delivered on the day after 14 days from its arrival in the
   box. gov.pl (20.10.2026): „termin na odpowiedź, odwołanie lub wykonanie określonego obowiązku
   zaczyna biec niezależnie od tego, czy przedsiębiorca faktycznie zapoznał się z treścią pisma”;
   email notification „ma charakter tylko informacyjny”. Tier 1 + 3. HIGH. Ania: yes.
10. **Who manages the box; access for the accountant.** Art. 19 ust. 2-3: a natural person may (need
    not) appoint a box administrator; the natural person and the administrator may manage the box.
    Art. 19 ust. 6 pkt 2: managing includes indicating natural persons authorised to send, receive
    and manage correspondence; ust. 7: they cannot authorise further people. gov.pl (20.10.2026):
    „Sama współpraca z biurem rachunkowym lub pełnomocnikiem nie oznacza automatycznego dostępu do
    korespondencji w e-Doręczeniach. Uprawnienia należy nadać odrębnie.” Tier 1 + 3. HIGH. Ania:
    yes.
11. **Activation is required.** biznes.gov.pl 004495: only after activation is the ADE entered in
    BAE; gov.pl 20.10.2026: filing the application alone does not meet the obligation. Activation in
    Konto Przedsiębiorcy (biznes.gov.pl) → e-Doręczenia → Aktywuj; notification email and Poczta
    Polska regulamin. Tier 3. HIGH.
12. **How to apply.** Art. 15 ust. 1 pkt 1: for CEIDG/KRS entities via the online service of the
    minister of economy (biznes.gov.pl e-usługa 00_0709_00); ust. 1a: also via the designated
    operator's online service. Also possible in a CEIDG change application. Signed with profil
    zaufany, podpis osobisty (e-dowód) or kwalifikowany (art. 14 ust. 3 pkt 1; biznes.gov.pl). A
    private ADE (as citizen) is not the company ADE; a JDG needs a separate company address
    (biznes.gov.pl). The company box from the public provider is used in Konto Przedsiębiorcy on
    biznes.gov.pl. Tier 1 + 3. HIGH. Note: gov.pl front page promotes e-Doręczenia in mObywatel app
    for citizens; for the company box biznes.gov.pl names Konto Przedsiębiorcy. Whether the company
    box is also visible in mObywatel not verified.
13. **Public vs qualified providers.** Public provider = designated operator, currently Poczta
    Polska S.A.; free for citizens and firms. Qualified providers (KDU) listed on biznes.gov.pl:
    Asseco Data Systems (Certum), Autenti, Poczta Polska (ePoczta), PWPW (edopost); paid per offer.
    Tier 3. HIGH. (Do not name/recommend providers in the post beyond „kwalifikowani dostawcy”.)
14. **Fees.** Art. 52 ust. 1: designated operator charges the sender; ust. 3: non-public → public
    via PURDE is exempt. gov.pl/oplaty: to a public body 0 zł; public body → firm PURDE 5,30 zł, PUH
    8,35 zł (paid by the public body); firm → firm via qualified provider per price list.
    biznes.gov.pl: obtaining the address is „bezpłatne”. Tier 1 + 3. HIGH.
15. **ePUAP end.** Art. 147 ust. 2-3 (t.j. 2026 poz. 3): ePUAP correspondence from non-public to
    public bodies (and replies back) was equivalent to PURDE only until 31.12.2025. gov.pl
    (11.12.2025): from 1.01.2026 e-Doręczenia are the basic channel unless specific provisions allow
    ePUAP; deemed delivery for ePUAP letters ends. Art. 147 ust. 2 refers to equivalence with PURDE
    delivery, not to a postal „przesyłka rejestrowana”. Art. 148: ePUAP inbox contents accessible
    for viewing/copying until 30.09.2029. Tier 1 + 3. HIGH.
16. **KSeF link (ZAW-FA).** ZAW-FA(3) can be filed on paper, via e-Urząd Skarbowy or e-Doręczenia;
    via ePUAP not effective since 1.01.2026. A JDG does not file ZAW-FA except to register its own
    qualified signature without NIP/PESEL. Source: sibling brief `logowanie-ksef.md` facts 10, 13
    (Podręcznik KSeF 2.0 cz. I, 06.08.2026, pp. 57-61). Tier 2. HIGH. Not re-fetched today.

## Unsettled Questions

- Whether US letters in KSeF/VAT matters go to e-Doręczenia or to the e-Urząd Skarbowy account in
  practice: art. 144 § 1a allows both („konto w systemie teleinformatycznym organu podatkowego”); no
  podatki.gov.pl page on e-Doręczenia found (podatki.gov.pl/e-doreczenia → 404). Do not claim
  „decyzje US przychodzą wyłącznie na e-Doręczenia”.
- What happens legally to a JDG that missed 30.09.2026: no sanction in the act; whether CEIDG or the
  minister takes any action is not stated in sources fetched.
- Whether the box is accessible in mObywatel for the company address (not verified).
- How art. 145 § 2 (pełnomocnik) interacts with the client's own ADE in practice: not described by
  official pages fetched.

## Warning: Common Misconceptions

- „Za brak e-Doręczeń jest kara” — not in the act (fact 5).
- „Termin to 1 października” — the act says entry obtained „do dnia 30 września 2026 r.”; gov.pl
  says obligation applies „od 1 października 2026 r.” Both describe the same moment.
- „Wystarczy złożyć wniosek” — the box must be activated (fact 11).
- „Biuro rachunkowe ma dostęp, bo ma pełnomocnictwo” — access must be granted separately (fact 10).
- „Prywatna skrzynka z mObywatela wystarczy dla firmy” — a separate company ADE is needed (fact 12).
- „ePUAP nadal działa do pism” — not effective for letters to public bodies since 1.01.2026 (fact
  15).
