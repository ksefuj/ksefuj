# Research Brief: Kary za błędy w KSeF: art. 106ni i projekt UD477

**Requested by:** weekly knowledge refresh **Date:** 2026-10-07 **Freshness:** 2026-10-07 **For
content:** update of posts that say "kary od 1 stycznia 2027" (see CONTENT IMPACT in the PR)

**Target query:** "kary KSeF od kiedy", "sankcje KSeF 2027", "UD477"

**Answer in two sentences:** In the law in force (consolidated VAT Act, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1263) the
financial penalty of art. 106ni applies from 1 January 2027. The Ministry of Finance announced on
2026-09-16 that it will defer it to the end of 2027 and put the draft UD477 (22 September 2026, not
yet enacted) into inter-ministerial consultation, which moves art. 106ni ust. 1–3 and 5–7 to 1
January 2028.

**What changes the answer:** whether UD477 is adopted and published in Dziennik Ustaw before 31
December 2026 (planned entry into force of the draft: 31 December 2026, art. 3 of the draft). Until
publication the binding text still says 1 January 2027.

## Key Facts (ready to use)

1. **Binding text today: penalty from 2027-01-01.** Art. 106ni VAT Act is marked „Wejdzie w życie z
   dniem 1 stycznia 2027 r." Penalty: „do 100 % kwoty podatku wykazanego na tej fakturze wystawionej
   poza Krajowym Systemem e-Faktur", and for invoices without tax „do 18,7 % kwoty należności
   ogółem". Imposed by the naczelnik urzędu skarbowego by decision; payable within 14 days (ust. 3).
   — Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1263, art. 106ni (https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/1263) Tier 1
   Confidence: HIGH Ania-relevant: yes
2. **The penalty article is 106ni, not 106gc.** Older briefs and posts cite „art. 106gc"; the
   penalty provision in the consolidated text is art. 106ni. — same source Confidence: HIGH
   Ania-relevant: yes (correctness of citations)
3. **Grounds (art. 106ni ust. 1):** (1) invoice not issued in KSeF despite the obligation; (2)
   during awaria/niedostępność/offline24, invoice issued not according to the published template
   (pkt 2 cites art. 106ne ust. 1 lub 3, ust. 4, art. 106nda ust. 1); (3) invoice not sent to KSeF
   in time (art. 106nda ust. 1, 106nf ust. 1, 106nh ust. 1). — same source Confidence: HIGH
4. **MF announcement 2026-09-16:** penalties for KSeF mistakes deferred to the end of 2027 (title:
   „Przedłużenie odroczenia kar za błędy w stosowaniu KSeF do końca 2027 r."). — MF,
   https://www.gov.pl/web/finanse/przedluzenie-odroczenia-kar-za-bledy-w-stosowaniu-ksef-do-konca-2027-r
   Tier 3 Confidence: HIGH (it is an announcement, not law) Ania-relevant: yes
5. **Draft UD477 (project of 2026-09-22), RCL: https://legislacja.rcl.gov.pl/projekt/12414954.** MF
   letter of 2026-09-23 (znak PT8.8100.2.2026) sends it to uzgodnienia; comments due 2026-09-30;
   reason for the short deadline: „pilnego wprowadzenia zmian w zakresie odroczenia stosowania kar w
   KSeF, które aktualnie miały obowiązywać od 1 stycznia 2027 r." Stage on 2026-10-07: uzgodnienia /
   konsultacje publiczne / opiniowanie (all opened 2026-09-23). Tier 1 (draft, not binding)
   Confidence: HIGH Ania-relevant: yes
6. **Content of UD477 (draft text):**
   - art. 2 pkt 1: in art. 106ni ust. 1 pkt 2 the words „lub 3" are struck;
   - art. 2 pkt 2: new art. 23 pkt 5 of the 2023 amending act: art. 1 pkt 24 „w zakresie art. 106ni
     ust. 1–3 i 5–7" enters into force on **1 January 2028**; pkt 4 (art. 1 pkt 30) stays 1 January
     2027;
   - art. 1 pkt 1: art. 106nd ust. 2 pkt 8 reworded („przydzielania fakturom ustrukturyzowanym
     numeru identyfikującego te faktury w Krajowym Systemie e-Faktur");
   - art. 1 pkt 2: art. 106nh ust. 4 cross-reference „art. 106nda ust. 4–15" -> „ust. 3–15";
   - art. 1 pkt 3: art. 145e ust. 1 (reverse charge on gas, electricity, emission allowances) „do
     dnia 31 grudnia 2026 r." -> „do dnia 30 czerwca 2030 r.";
   - art. 3: entry into force 31 December 2026. Confidence: HIGH (draft text) Ania-relevant: partly
7. **From the draft's uzasadnienie (per MF/legislative summary, verify in the RCL file before
   quoting):** from 1.01.2027 the 10 000 zł exemption (art. 145m) and cash-register invoices (art.
   145n) are not available; KAS intends to remind first, then verify (art. 272 Ordynacja podatkowa).
   Confidence: MEDIUM
8. **MF FAQ „Najczęstsze pytania" (modified 29.04.2026):** „za 2026 rok nie będą nakładane sankcje
   za błędy popełnione podczas korzystania z KSeF" (Q2) and penalties „dopiero za naruszenia
   dokonane od 1 stycznia 2027 roku" (Q3, citing art. 106ni). Q3 is now outdated if UD477 is
   adopted. — https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/ksef-news/najczestsze-pytania/ Tier 3 Confidence: HIGH

## Unsettled Questions

- Will UD477 be enacted and published by 31 December 2026? Do NOT state 2028 as law until it is
  published; say "zapowiedziane przez MF, projekt w uzgodnieniach".
- Does the deferral to 2028 also move art. 106ni ust. 4 (penalty for not issuing offline24 invoice
  etc.)? The draft keeps ust. 4 outside the deferred list; read the consolidated text before
  claiming anything about ust. 4.
- Whether MF will publish an updated FAQ answer for Q2/Q3 and the legal-basis page.

## Warning: Common Misconceptions

- „Kary od 1 stycznia 2027" is the law as of 2026-10-07 but MF has announced a deferral; write both.
- „Brak kar = brak obowiązku": wrong. Obligation stands since 1.04.2026 (1.02.2026 for large); an
  invoice outside KSeF is a formal defect (MF FAQ Q4), and the deduction right is kept if art. 86 is
  met (Q1).
- „Kary to art. 106gc": wrong, art. 106ni.
