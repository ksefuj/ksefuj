# Research Brief: Kary za błędy w KSeF: art. 106ni i projekt UD477

**Requested by:** weekly knowledge refresh **Date:** 2026-10-07 **Freshness:** 2026-11-07 **For
content:** update of posts that say "kary od 1 stycznia 2027" (see CONTENT IMPACT in the PR)

**Target query:** "kary KSeF od kiedy", "sankcje KSeF 2027", "UD477"

**Answer in two sentences:** In the law in force (consolidated VAT Act, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1263) the
financial penalty of art. 106ni applies from 1 January 2027. The Ministry of Finance announced on
2026-09-16 that it will defer it to the end of 2027; the government bill UD477 (Sejm print 3245),
which moves art. 106ni ust. 1–3 and 5–7 to 1 January 2028, was passed by the Sejm on 2026-11-06
without amendments (437 for, 1 against, 1 abstaining) and now goes to the Senate. It is not yet law
(no Senate vote, no signature, not published).

**What changes the answer:** whether the act passed by the Sejm gets through the Senate and the
President and is published in Dziennik Ustaw before 31 December 2026 (entry into force: 31 December
2026, art. 3). Until publication the binding text still says 1 January 2027.

  as law until it is published; say "Sejm uchwalił 6 listopada 2026 r., ustawa czeka na Senat i
  Prezydenta".
- The Senate druk number, Senate vote and any Senate amendments (which would send the act back to
  the Sejm) are unknown as of 2026-11-07.
- The official text after third reading (3245_u3.pdf) was not retrievable (HTTP 404); re-check and
  quote from it once available. sejm.gov.pl pages return 403 to non-browser clients.
- Does the deferral to 2028 also move art. 106ni ust. 4 (penalty for not issuing offline24 invoice

  claiming anything about ust. 4.

## Warning: Common Misconceptions

- „Kary od 1 stycznia 2027" is the law as of 2026-10-07 but MF has announced a deferral; write both.
- „Brak kar = brak obowiązku": wrong. Obligation stands since 1.04.2026 (1.02.2026 for large); an
  invoice outside KSeF is a formal defect (MF FAQ Q4), and the deduction right is kept if art. 86 is
  met (Q1).
- „Kary to art. 106gc": wrong, art. 106ni.
- „Sejm uchwalił, więc kary są od 2028": not yet. As of 2026-11-07 the act still needs the Senate,
  the President's signature and publication.
- „PiS chciał zwolnić z KSeF firmy wystawiające faktury do 10 000 zł miesięcznie" (press summaries):
  the amendment text (druk 3286-A) covers mikroprzedsiębiorcy (Prawo przedsiębiorców, art. 7 ust. 1
  pkt 1) from 1.12.2026 to 31.12.2027, with no 10 000 zł threshold. It was rejected.
